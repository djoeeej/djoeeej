## 1. Emigration, brain drain and return migration: what new or under-the-radar tools work?

### Takeaway
The strongest causal evidence for a return-migration tool comes from **Italy's 2010 returnee tax break**. About a quarter of eligible returnees would not have come back without it, and the scheme can pay for itself if returnees stay. **Poland's 2022 "PIT-0 for returnees"** is scaling fast but has not been evaluated. Low-cost **Latvian regional "remigration coordinators"** are a close Baltic analogue with modest but steady results. **Portugal's IRS Jovem** youth tax cut is costly, and the IMF (2026) found no clear evidence that it works. Relocation-cash schemes such as **Tulsa Remote** report high retention, but only in self-published data.

### Cited Findings

#### Latvia: regional remigration coordinators (2018–present)
- A pilot run by Latvia's environment and regional development ministry had helped **64 emigrant families return by end of July 2018**. — [Baltic Times](https://www.baltictimes.com/remigration_pilot_project_has_brought_64_families_back_to_latvia); [LSM](https://eng.lsm.lv/article/society/latvians-abroad/remigration-pilot-project-has-brought-64-families-back-to-latvia.a290679/)
- In the first eight months, coordinators contacted about **1,300 emigrant families, of whom about 10% returned**. — [OECD International Migration Outlook 2019](https://oecd.org/en/publications/international-migration-outlook-2019_c3e35eec-en/full-report/component-29)
- In 2020, **Vidzeme recorded 66 returning families** and **Latgale 83 (up from 64 in 2019)**, despite COVID travel restrictions. — [LSM 2020](https://eng.lsm.lv/article/society/latvians-abroad/many-families-with-children-returned-to-live-in-latvia-in-2020.a388591/)
- Regional results: 30 families returned to Latgale and 23 to Kurzeme in early phases. — [Baltic Times – Latgale](https://www.baltictimes.com/30_families_have_returned_to_latgale_as_part_of_re-emigration_project/); [Baltic Times – Kurzeme](https://www.baltictimes.com/23_families_have_returned_to_kurzeme_as_part_of_re-emigration_project/)
- Families mostly ask about **school support for children who do not speak Latvian, business re-registration and diploma recognition**. — [LSM](https://eng.lsm.lv/article/society/latvians-abroad/re-emigration-project-has-helped-20-families-return-to-latvia-so-far.a283189/)
- **256 people returned to the Riga region via the coordinator in 2024**, and more than **1,600 people had returned to Riga and the Riga area as of January 2025**. In 2024, more returnees cited **rising living costs abroad** as a reason. — [Baltic Times 2025](https://www.baltictimes.com/more_than_1_600_people_have_returned_to_riga_and_the_riga_area_as_part_of_the_re-migration_coordinator_program/)
- A ministry slide (undated) cites 399 persons and about 173 families remigrated at a later stage. — [VASAB presentation](https://vasab.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/Presentation_2_Raivis_Bremsmits.pdf)
- No national cumulative total or counterfactual evaluation was found.

#### Poland: "Ulga na powrót" (PIT-0 for returnees, since 2022)
- Returnees are exempt from personal income tax on up to **PLN 85,528 of income per year for four years**. — [powroty.gov.pl (official)](https://powroty.gov.pl/pit-zero-dla-powracajacych/)
- Uptake: **9,353 people in 2022 (PLN 393m of income deducted)** and **about 18,100 people in 2023 (PLN 920m deducted)**. Press citing Finance Ministry data reported a **record number of beneficiaries in 2024**, and an economist called the scheme's premise "misguided" (chybione założenie). — [Interia Biznes](https://biznes.interia.pl/finanse/news-tusk-kontynuuje-program-pis-ekonomista-chybione-zalozenie,nId,22455761); [Infor](https://ksiegowosc.infor.pl/tematy/ulga-na-powrot/)
- The relief has been kept under the Tusk government (2024–). — [Interia Biznes](https://biznes.interia.pl/finanse/news-tusk-kontynuuje-program-pis-ekonomista-chybione-zalozenie,nId,22455761)
- No causal evaluation was found that separates induced returns from windfalls to people who would have returned anyway.

#### Italy: returnee tax incentives ("Controesodo" 2010, precursor of today's "impatriati" regime)
- Bassetto & Ippedico (CESifo WP, 2023): eligible people (college graduates born in 1969 or later) were **27% more likely to move back to Italy after the 2010 reform**. An earlier draft estimated 50%. The direct fiscal effect was **marginally positive**, partly because the scheme targeted young workers. — [CESifo 2023](https://cesifo.org/en/publications/2023/working-paper/can-tax-incentives-bring-brains-back-returnees-tax-schemes-and-high); [UC Davis GMC](https://globalmigration.ucdavis.edu/node/6516)
- The February 2026 version finds that about **one-quarter of eligible post-2010 returnees would not have returned without the scheme**, implying an **elasticity of migration flows to the net-of-tax rate of 0.9**. The scheme is **costly short-term but can pay for itself in present value if enough returnees stay** after it ends. — [RFBerlin WP 2026](https://www.rfberlin.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/26097.pdf)
- German social-security data (IAB project) show **significant effects throughout the wage distribution**, not just among top earners. Lower-wage Italian emigrants in Germany were *more* likely to return, which is consistent with underemployment abroad. — [IAB Forum](https://iab-forum.de/en/?p=34807); [IAB project page](https://iab.de/project/?id=1784542)

#### Portugal: IRS Jovem (youth income-tax relief; expanded 2025)
- A NOVA SBE evaluation rates IRS Jovem **"probably ineffective and intergenerationally unfair"**. Even the closest destination, Spain, paid an average **€25,671.89 in 2024, 44% above Portugal in PPP terms**, and the tax cut cannot close that gap. — [NOVA SBE Economics for Policy](https://economicsforpolicy.novasbe.pt/publications/intergenerational-policy-evaluation/youth-income-tax-irs-jovem/)
- **IMF post-programme mission (May 2026)** recommended reversing it, citing high fiscal cost, distortions and **no clear evidence of curbing youth emigration**. — [Sábado](https://www.sabado.pt/dinheiro/detalhe/fmi-recomenda-fim-do-irs-jovem-devido-aos-custos-fiscais-e-distorcoes-que-causa)
- Finance Minister Miranda Sarmento replied that IRS Jovem and public mortgage guarantees for young buyers reduce emigration probability "considerably", and called keeping it "a political choice". — [ECO, 13 May 2026](https://eco.sapo.pt/2026/05/13/sarmento-responde-as-criticas-do-fmi-sobre-irs-jovem-e-uma-escolha-politica-do-governo/)
- A proposal by PM Montenegro extended relief to **ages 18–35 earning up to €28,000/year for 10 years** (pending parliament at the time of reporting). — [Xataka Brasil](https://www.xataka.com.br/diversos/portugal-tomou-uma-decisao-desesperada-diante-da-fuga-cerebros-uma-decada-sem-impostos-para-quem-ficar)

#### Tulsa Remote (Oklahoma, USA): paid relocation for remote workers (2018–)
- An earlier study found that **76% of members who joined since 2019 and completed their programme year still lived in Tulsa at end-2022**. — [HR Brew 2023](https://www.hr-brew.com/stories/2023/06/22/most-tulsa-remote-workers-haven-t-left); [Route Fifty](https://www.route-fifty.com/workforce/2023/06/who-wants-live-tulsa-turns-out-lot-people/388133/)
- For 2022, each member generated **>$150,937 in labour income (salary plus induced local wages), about 12× programme expenses**. — [Tulsa Remote Economic Impact Report (2024 PDF)](https://ddc.downtowndevelopment.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Tulsa-Remote-Economic-Impact-Report.pdf)
- 2024 self-report: **$622m in economic contribution, $30.5m in county and state taxes, 1,400+ jobs**. — [Tulsa Remote impact report](https://landing.tulsaremote.com/economic-impact-report)
- 2025 report: **98% completed the programme year**, **80% two-year retention** (survey), and **3,972 "Remoters" generating $878m in direct employment income as of Dec 2025**. — [Tulsa Remote 2025 report (PDF)](https://landing.tulsaremote.com/hubfs/Tulsa%20Remote%202025%20Economic%20Impact%20Report.pdf?hsLang=en)
- Retention figures differ by definition: 90% (2024 podcast, 3,000+ members) vs 95% "since 2019" (funder, May 2025). — [DCI podcast](https://aboutdci.com/blog/talent-wars-podcast-do-incentives-really-attract-talent/); [George Kaiser Family Foundation](https://www.gkff.org/stories/new-study-affirms-tulsa-remotes-impact)
- Caveat: all impact data are published by the programme or its funder, with no independent counterfactual.

#### Ponta do Sol, Madeira: "digital nomad village" (2021–)
- The Startup Madeira pilot ran for six months in 2021 and the regional government calls the results "overwhelming" (no figures published). The project was still active in 2025. — [Startup Madeira](https://digitalnomads.startupmadeira.eu/?p=3347)
- The founder self-reports **20 people at launch (Feb 2021) and over 2,500 digital nomads across the island by April 2021** (unaudited). — [Gonçalo Hall Substack](https://goncalohall.substack.com/p/what-is-a-destination-architect-the)
- Capacity was about 100 nomads in the village co-working. It was intended to offset lost tourism, which was cited at about 20% of regional GDP. — [TravelPulse](https://www.travelpulse.com/news/destinations/portugals-madeira-islands-create-worlds-first-digital-nomad-village.html)
- **No independent economic impact data was found.**

### Inferences
| Solution | How known | Evidence | Lithuania fit / analogue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Latvia remigration coordinators | Little-known outside Baltics | Monitoring counts only | Very high fit, since the diaspora and municipal structure are similar. Lithuania should check whether its municipal/"Global Lithuania" contact points do the same hands-on case management (schooling for non-Lithuanian-speaking kids, diploma recognition, business re-registration). A coordinator per region costs little. |
| Poland PIT-0 for returnees | Little-known outside Poland | Uptake data, no causal study | Plausible, but the Italian evidence suggests targeting (age, degree) and a sunset with stay-on rules improve fiscal payback. Poland's PLN 920m deducted in 2023 hints at large deadweight. |
| Italy returnee tax regime (2010 version) | Moderately known | Quasi-experimental (strongest in the set) | The best-evidenced design template if Lithuania wants a tax lever. Key features: young graduates, time-limited, and judged on whether people stay after expiry. |
| Portugal IRS Jovem | Moderately known | Critical evaluations (NOVA SBE, IMF) | A **cautionary** example. A broad youth tax cut is expensive and too small to offset wage gaps with destination countries. |
| Tulsa Remote | Moderately known (US) | Self-published evaluation | Could be adapted for regional towns (e.g., Lithuanian county centres) to attract remote workers and diaspora. Needs housing and community onboarding, not just cash. |
| Ponta do Sol nomad village | Widely known as a story | Anecdotal | Low evidence. Treat as place-marketing, not a demographic fix. |
- Common thread: the programmes with measured returns combine **money with human case management** (Latvia) or **targeted tax relief evaluated against a counterfactual** (Italy).

### Gaps
- No search results covered Ireland's/Croatia's/Romania's return programmes, Estonia's "Talendid koju", diaspora bonds or "boomerang" job platforms. The search budget ran out before they could be checked.
- The final 2025 IRS Jovem rules (age cap 35, sliding exemption over 10 years) are known only from the proposal coverage above and were not verified against the statute.
- Tulsa Remote's cash amount (widely reported as US$10,000) was **not verified** in this session.
- Poland's 2024 beneficiary count and the fiscal cost (tax forgone, as opposed to income deducted) were not found.
- Whether Lithuania already runs Latvia-style regional coordinators could not be verified.

## 2. Rural depopulation, shrinking towns, rural services and transport

### Takeaway
Japan's **Chiiki Okoshi Kyoryokutai** (paid urban-to-rural "revitalisation cooperators") now has a large-scale track record: **70% of 8,762 finishers stayed in or near the host area**. Spain's corporate-funded **Holapueblo** shows matchmaking works but at small scale (about 150 families). France's **France Services** one-stop rural hubs (more than 2,840 sites, over 90% satisfaction) are the best-evidenced service-access model. Demand-responsive rural transport has **mixed** evidence and must be designed carefully. Ireland's network of 400 remote-working hubs has no impact evaluation.

### Cited Findings

#### Japan: Chiiki Okoshi Kyoryokutai (regional revitalisation cooperation volunteers)
- An MIC survey (published April 2026) found that of **8,762 members whose terms ended between April 2020 and March 2025, 70.3% stayed in the area**: **57.5% in the same municipality** and **12.8% in a neighbouring one**. About 23% moved away. — [Institute for Social Vision Design column](https://isvd.or.jp/en/columns/kyoryokutai-settlement-575)
- **FY2025 membership was 8,196, up 286 year on year.** — [ISVD](https://isvd.or.jp/en/columns/kyoryokutai-settlement-575)
- Programme documentation is published by the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (Soumu). — [Soumu PDF](https://www.soumu.go.jp/main_content/000376274.pdf)

#### Spain: Holapueblo (Redeia + AlmaNatura + IKEA, with Triodos Bank; 2021–)
- The platform matches would-be rural entrepreneurs with depopulated municipalities and provides business mentoring. The EU Rural Pact reports **15,000+ applications, 130+ municipalities engaged and 150+ families relocated**. Participants are mostly **Spanish couples around 40 with children**, and **71% of the businesses are in services**. — [EU Rural Pact good practice](https://ruralpact.rural-vision.europa.eu/good-practice/holapueblo-connecting-people-rural-municipalities-revitalise-depopulated-areas-spain_en)
- **Mid-2023: 133 people (51 families) settled in 34 towns.** — [Redeia press release Jul 2023](https://www.redeia.com/en/sala-de-prensa/actualidad/nota-de-prensa/2023/07/33-municipalities-depopulated-Spain-receive-133-inhabitants-with-support-Redeia-IKEA-AlmaNatura)
- **Oct 2024: 45 towns gained 167 residents (68 families), with 41 more people (20 families) expected.** — [Redeia press release Oct 2024](https://www.redeia.com/en/press-office/news/press-release/2024/10/nearly-50-depopulated-towns-in-Spain-gain-new-residents-with-holapueblo)
- A 4th edition reported 148 municipalities and 114 families assisted. — [Corresponsables](https://www.corresponsables.com/actualidad/actualidadiv-edicion-de-holapueblo-para-reactivar-y-repoblar-la-espana-rural/)

#### France: France Services (one-stop public-service hubs, 2019/2020–)
- A Cour des comptes evaluation (Sept 2024, programme 2020–2023) found a "quantitative and qualitative gain", **user satisfaction above 90%** and **more than 2,840 sites**. The court asked for the budget to be strengthened. — [Le Monde du Droit](https://www.lemondedudroit.fr/public/282-droit-public-general/94465-cour-des-comptes-programme-france-services-2020-2023.html); [MoneyVox](https://www.moneyvox.fr/actu/99379/impots-hlm-caf-la-cour-des-comptes-demande-de-renforcer-le-budget-de-france-services)
- The government's target is **3,000 sites by 2027**. — [Transformation.gouv.fr report](https://www.transformation.gouv.fr/files/presse/Frances%20services%20rapport.pdf)
- 2025 Services Publics+ indicators: **76% overall satisfaction**, 79% for welcoming reception, 73% for ease of contact and 74% for processing times. — [Services Publics+ ANCT](https://www.plus.transformation.gouv.fr/anct)
- For comparison, overall satisfaction across 19 French public services was 69% (June 2025, 25,000 users). — [Maire-info](https://www.maire-info.com/services-publics/69--franais-sont-satisfaits-services-publics-selon-un-barometre-du-ministere-la-fonction-publique-article-29755)

#### Demand-responsive transport (DRT) in low-density areas
- **Amsterdam Mokumflex** (an on-demand service replacing two bus lines) saw a **65% drop in passengers** over 12 months, and **only 16% of trips were shared**. — [Thredbo 16 workshop paper](https://thredbo-conference-series.org/downloads/thredbo16/Workshop-4.pdf)
- A later before-and-after study of a DRT pilot reported **89% fewer passenger-km** and about **94% satisfaction** among on-time trips. — [US DOT ITS KRS 2025](https://www.itskrs.its.dot.gov/2025-b01958)
- **"Sprinti" (Hannover region, Lower Saxony)** recorded about **570,000 rides in its first year**. The EU DREAM_PACE project runs rural and mountain pilots (e.g., East Tyrol). — [EU Urban Mobility Observatory](https://urban-mobility-observatory.transport.ec.europa.eu/strategic-planning-demand-responsive-transport-insights-european-projects_en)
- The **Harz "EcoBus" rural pilot** (Aug 2018–Feb 2019) used up to 10 vans and was the basis for analysis of ride-pooling sustainability thresholds. — [arXiv 2306.05851](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.05851)
- England's **Rural Mobility Fund** (rural and suburban DRT pilots): interim evaluation 2023, **final report published Dec 2025** (figures not retrieved). — [GOV.UK final report](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/rural-mobility-fund-phase-1-evaluation-final-report); [interim](https://www.gov.uk:443/government/publications/rural-mobility-fund-evaluation-interim-report)

#### Ireland: Connected Hubs and remote-working hubs (Our Rural Future 2021–2025)
- The policy targeted a **network of 400 remote-working hubs** and the **400th hub was onboarded**. The network launched in May 2021 with about 60 hubs and began as a pilot of the Western Development Commission. — [gov.ie 400th hub](https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-rural-and-community-development-and-the-gaeltacht/press-releases/key-milestone-reached-as-minister-calleary-announces-onboarding-of-the-400th-connected-hub); [WDC](https://westerndevelopment.ie/?p=10108)
- In July 2021, **€8.8m** went to about 117–118 hub projects, alongside a separate **€5m Connected Hubs Fund**. — [gov.ie €8.8m](https://www.gov.ie/ga/preasraitis/a6253-our-rural-future-minister-humphreys-announces-88-million-in-funding-under-the-connected-hubs-scheme/); [gov.ie €5m](https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-rural-and-community-development-and-the-gaeltacht/press-releases/our-rural-future-minister-humphreys-announces-5-million-connected-hubs-fund/); [Agriland](https://www.agriland.ie/?p=640228)
- **No independent outcome evaluation was found.** Aims such as population retention and town-centre revival are stated, not measured.

### Inferences
| Solution | How known | Evidence | Lithuania fit / analogue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chiiki Okoshi Kyoryokutai | Little-known outside Japan | Large-n administrative follow-up (no control group) | **Strong candidate.** A state-paid 1–3-year contract for young urban (or diaspora) people to work on municipal development projects in small towns, with settlement as the goal. Could be piloted with EU ESF+/LEADER money in the most depopulating districts (e.g., Ignalina, Zarasai, Pagėgiai). |
| Holapueblo | Little-known | Programme counts | A cheap, corporate-CSR-funded matchmaking model. A Lithuanian version could partner energy/telecom companies with LEADER local action groups. Expect small numbers, roughly dozens of families per year. |
| France Services | Moderately known | Audit-court evaluation | High fit. Bundle Sodra, VMI, Užimtumo tarnyba, Registrų centras, health insurance and post counters in one staffed rural point (could reuse post offices or seniūnijos, the local elderships). |
| Rural DRT | Moderately known | Mixed; some failures | Pilot only with low-fare, scheduled-plus-flex hybrids. Mokumflex shows ridership can collapse when DRT *replaces* lines. |
| Ireland remote hubs | Little-known | None | Cheap and quick to build. Pair with return-migration outreach, and measure take-up. |

### Gaps
- No sources were found for Japan's furusato nōzei latest totals, Finland/Sweden mobile service units, rural co-working in Spain beyond Holapueblo, or Germany's Dorfladen co-ops (search budget exhausted).
- Kyoryokutai per-member national funding levels and cost per settled person were not found.
- France Services' design rules (e.g., the 30-minute access target, the list of partner agencies, mobile "France Services" buses) were not verified.
- The England Rural Mobility Fund final-report ridership and cost-per-trip figures were not retrieved.

## 3. Low fertility and family policy

### Takeaway
Only one item could be verified this session. **Nagi, Japan** shows that a bundle of cash, childcare, housing and medical subsidies in a single small town correlated with fertility rising from about 1.4 to 2.95 (2019), later about 2.4. This is a correlation in a tiny population, not causal evidence. National evidence on Hungary, Korea, Estonia, employer schemes and Nordic childcare could not be gathered.

### Cited Findings

#### Nagi town (Okayama, Japan): the "miracle town"
- The total fertility rate roughly **doubled from about 1.4 (2005) to about 2.8 (2014)** and hit **2.95 in 2019**. It later dipped to **2.39**, against a national average of **1.46** (reported 2023–2024). — [Arab News Japan](https://www.arabnews.jp/en/japan/article_90026); [Jiji](https://sp.m.jiji.com/english/show/24809)
- The policy bundle includes:
  - daycare for the first child at about half the national standard, with discounts for later children
  - school-lunch subsidies and free learning materials
  - medical costs not covered by insurance paid for children up to high-school age
  - cash per birth of ¥100,000 for the first child, ¥150,000 for the second and up to ¥400,000 for the fifth
  - subsidised family housing at about ¥50,000/month
  - funding by cutting the number of town officials and assembly members

  — [Fox13/CNN 2019](https://www.fox13now.com/2019/02/03/inside-the-japanese-town-that-pays-cash-for-kids); [Arab News Japan](https://www.arabnews.jp/en/japan/article_90026)
- **PM Kishida visited** to inform national birth-rate measures. — [Jiji](https://sp.m.jiji.com/english/show/24809)

### Inferences
| Solution | How known | Evidence | Lithuania fit / analogue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nagi bundle | Moderately known in Japan, little-known elsewhere | Descriptive trend (small-n, volatile) | Suggests that **municipal-level bundles** (childcare price, housing for young families, child health costs) are feasible for Lithuanian rural municipalities. Expect migration of families between towns as much as extra births. |

### Gaps
- **Not researched (search budget exhausted):** Hungary's family-policy evaluations; South Korea's 2024–2025 birth uptick and measures (Booyoung Group bonuses, Incheon's schemes); employer-based fertility support (e.g., Itochu's reported in-house fertility rise after a morning-work shift); Estonia's 2017 large-family allowance; and Nordic childcare evidence. These are leads only and need verification before use.
- Nagi's current (2023–2025) TFR and town population were not verified, and with a population of a few thousand, TFR swings are statistically noisy.

## 4. Aging, elder care, loneliness and social isolation

### Takeaway
The best-evidenced item is Japan's **kayoi-no-ba** (community gathering places for exercise and socialising). Frequent participants had about 70% lower odds of needing long-term care (observational data). **AI companions** (Korea's Hyodol doll, NY State's ElliQ) show promising but mostly self-reported or uncontrolled results. **Seoul's 24/7 loneliness hotline** passed its annual demand target in six weeks. Low-cost social designs such as **Jumbo's "Kletskassa" chat checkouts** and **St. Gallen's Zeitvorsorge time-banking** scale cheaply but lack outcome evaluations.

### Cited Findings

#### Japan: kayoi-no-ba (municipal community "gathering places" for older adults)
- Watanabe et al. (2025, *J Public Health*) followed **9,382 residents aged 65+ without long-term-care (LTC) certification in FY2019, through 2019–2022**. About **16% participated**. Those attending **100+ times had adjusted OR 0.28 (95% CI 0.14–0.59) for new LTC certification** compared with non-participants. Higher attendance tracked **lower LTC benefit costs**, but medical expenses did not differ significantly. — [OUCI listing of the article](https://ouci.dntb.gov.ua/works/4M11p3eb)
- The AGES/JAGES cohort (Takahashi et al., 2019, *BMJ Open*) followed **15,313 people across six municipalities for 9.4 years**, examining social participation against future LTC need and mortality. — [BMJ Open](https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/9/11/e030500)
- JAGES has published a WHO Kobe Centre policy brief on social participation and healthy ageing. — [WHO Kobe policy brief](https://extranet.who.int/kobe_centre/sites/default/files/pdf/201902_JAGES_policy_brief_en_2.pdf)
- A review chapter describes kayoi-no-ba as a **local-government-led preventive care initiative** offering regular social participation and physical activity. — [IntechOpen 2025](https://www.intechopen.com/online-first/1226629)

#### South Korea: Hyodol AI companion doll (deployed via local governments)
- A preliminary peer-reviewed study of **278 community-dwelling older adults in medically underserved areas, over 6 months**, found depressive symptoms fell significantly and the **share at high risk of depression dropped 45%**. Loneliness and medication adherence improved. There was a **dose-response** pattern: more chatting meant larger reductions. The authors say **controlled trials are needed**. — [PMC12786537](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12786537/)
- In a company-run study of about **1,230 people living alone**, the high-risk depression group shrank **35.7%** and the high-risk loneliness group **24.7%**. About **7,000 dolls** had been deployed. Depression is **about 1.5× more common among seniors living alone** (KIHASA). Some users felt discomfort when the doll misunderstood them. — [Asia News Network / Korea Herald](https://asianews.network/can-ai-robots-replace-family-in-south-korea/); [Inquirer](https://globalnation.inquirer.net/?p=314236)

#### New York State Office for the Aging (NYSOFA) + ElliQ (Intuition Robotics), 2022–

## 5. Suicide prevention, men's mental health and youth substance prevention

### Takeaway
The highest-value transferable model is **ASSIP**, a three-session brief therapy after a suicide attempt. In a Swiss RCT, the ASSIP group had **5 reattempts over 24 months against 41 under usual care**, and the therapy was cost-saving. Lithuania already has feasibility work on it. Workplace and sector peer models (**MATES in Construction**, NZ **Farmstrong**) and men's peer groups (**Andy's Man Club**) are scaling with promising but weak causal evidence. **AI risk prediction (US VA REACH VET)** has poor accuracy for suicide death. **Planet Youth** (the Icelandic model) is already being used in Vilnius.

### Cited Findings

#### ASSIP: Attempted Suicide Short Intervention Program (Switzerland → Finland, Sweden, Lithuania, US)
- In the original Swiss RCT, the ASSIP group had **80% fewer reattempts and 72% fewer hospital days over 24 months** than usual care: **5 attempts vs 41**. — [PMC10910170](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10910170)
- An LSE cost-effectiveness analysis (120 patients) found ASSIP **more effective and less costly** than usual care plus a single risk assessment. — [LSE news](https://www.lse.ac.uk/news/latest-news-from-lse/short-suicide-prevention-programme-more-effective-and-cost-effective-than-alternative-care)
- A Finnish RCT (Helsinki–Uusimaa) compares ASSIP with crisis counselling, but no results were found. — [BMJ Open protocol](https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/13/3/e070105.full.pdf); [ISRCTN13464512](https://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN13464512)
- A US hybrid effectiveness–implementation RCT in New York State is under way with 400 adults after a recent attempt. — [SUNY](https://researchconnect.suny.edu/en/publications/effectiveness-of-a-targeted-brief-intervention-for-recent-suicide/); [Suicideinfo.ca](https://www.suicideinfo.ca/resource/effectiveness-of-a-targeted-brief-intervention-for-recent-suicide-attempt-survivors-a-randomised-controlled-trial-protocol/)
- ASSIP is reported as **implemented in Sweden, Finland and Lithuania**. A **Lithuanian feasibility study (Vilnius University)** examined the patient perspective and noted that most interventions were tested in low-suicide-rate countries. — [PMC9990685](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9990685); [VU ePublications](https://epublications.vu.lt/object/elaba:69830384/index.html)

#### MATES in Construction (Australia; spinoffs MATES in Energy and MATES in Manufacturing)
- A systematic review found **favourable results for reduced suicide risk**, but **limited causal inference**: very few controlled evaluations and no experimental studies. — [PMC10468011](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10468011/)
- In Queensland, the male construction-worker suicide rate fell **7.9% between 2003–07 and 2008–12** while the state male rate rose, but this was **not statistically significant**. Lesser-skilled workers saw a 22.5% fall, also n.s. — [PMC10468011](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10468011/); earlier evaluation [PMC3228565](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3228565)
- **MATES in Manufacturing** was tested in a **cluster RCT across 10 worksites** with help-seeking intentions as the primary outcome. — [PMC11898168](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11898168/)
- A Northern Territory counterfactual costing model projects fewer suicides (modelled, not measured). — [BMC Psychiatry](https://link.springer.com/10.1186/s12888-019-2142-3)

#### Andy's Man Club (UK peer-support groups for men, 2016–)
- The club runs **weekly 2-hour peer groups** where men can listen without having to speak. Feb 2025: **over 270 groups attended by about 5,800 men**, with another count of 320+ venues. In March 2024, over 4,500 men attended weekly. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy%27s_Man_Club)
- The Scottish Government's practice stories report **1,600 volunteers, 170+ groups and an average of 4,300+ men/week** (earlier stage). — [gov.scot](https://gov.scot/publications/time-space-compassion-suicide-prevention-practice-stories-volume-2/pages/5)
- **No formal outcome evaluation was found.**

#### Farmstrong (New Zealand rural and farmer wellbeing programme)
- Its 2024 evaluation report found **high value relative to resourcing**: over 2020–2025, **17–22% of surveyed farmers and growers attributed improved wellbeing to Farmstrong, an estimated 16,000–21,000 people/year**. — [Farmstrong Evaluation Report 2024](https://farmstrong.co.nz/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Evaluation_Report_2024.pdf)
- July 2024–June 2025: **23% of all NZ farmers (about 20,000)** attributed improvement, and **83% of those who engaged** did. — [Farmstrong annual results](https://farmstrong.co.nz/farmstrong-annual-results-show-positive-impact/)
- The 2023 figure was 18% (about 14,000). — [Farmstrong 2023 infographic](https://farmstrong.co.nz/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Farmstrong_Infographic_Annual_Results_2023.pdf)
- Caveat: this is self-reported attribution, published by the programme.

#### US Veterans Health Administration: REACH VET (machine-learning suicide-risk flagging)
- For suicide death, the original model had a **positive predictive value of 0.05% and a false-negative rate of 98.1%**. For legally involved veterans the figures were 0.10% and 82.4%. — [PMC12535588](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12535588/); [Council on Criminal Justice](https://counciloncj.org/can-suicide-be-predicted-for-justice-involved-veterans/)
- The programme evaluation found a **5% reduction in suicide attempts but no reduction in suicide deaths**. — [Psychiatric Services](https://psychiatryonline.org/doi/10.1176/appi.ps.202000092)
- **REACH VET 2.0** had a **C-statistic of 0.76 vs 0.69** and, in the top 0.1% of predicted risk, identified **5.6 vs 3.6 deaths/month** (developer validation). — [Suicideinfo.ca summary](https://www.suicideinfo.ca/resource/predictive-analytics-to-direct-clinical-attention-to-complex-patients-with-elevated-suicide-risk-enhancement-of-the-veterans-health-administration-reach-vet-model/)

#### Planet Youth (Icelandic Prevention Model): already in Lithuania
- **The Vilnius Public Health Bureau uses the Planet Youth model.** Student surveys identify risk and protective factors, schools build tailored prevention plans and get funding for mental-health activities, and delivery runs mainly through existing bureau staff. — [Interreg Europe good practice](https://www.interregeurope.eu/good-practices/services-provided-by-public-health-bureaus-in-lithuania-to-strengthen-youth-mental-health)
- **Planet Youth began new partnerships in 2025 in Ireland, Lithuania, Germany, France, the US and Canada.** — [Planet Youth year in perspective](https://www.dbrecoveryresources.com/?p=121932)
- In Iceland, the share of 10th-graders drunk in the past 30 days fell from **42% (1998) to under 5% (2018)**. — [NHS Highland Planet Youth description](https://www.highlandsubstanceawareness.scot.nhs.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Planet-Youth-Project-Description.pdf)
- An Irish review questions whether the model transfers to other contexts and notes its **commercial nature and resource needs**, calling for a stronger evidence base. — [Drugs and Alcohol (Ireland)](https://www.drugsandalcohol.ie/39495/)

### Inferences
| Solution | How known | Evidence | Lithuania fit / analogue |
|---|---|---|---|
| ASSIP | Little-known outside suicidology | RCT + cost-effectiveness | **Top priority.** Lithuania has feasibility work. The gap is likely routine rollout in every emergency department and psychiatric service after an attempt. |
| MATES in Construction / Manufacturing | Moderately known | Observational + 1 cluster RCT (help-seeking) | High fit for Lithuania's male-dominated construction, logistics, forestry and agriculture sectors. Industry-funded models (Australia) reduce state cost. |
| Andy's Man Club | Little-known outside UK | None formal | Cheap volunteer model aimed at men who avoid clinical services. Pilot with evaluation from the start. |
| Farmstrong | Little-known | Self-reported programme evaluation | A rural Lithuanian version could be run with farmers' unions and an insurer. Focus on wellbeing, not "mental illness" framing. |
| REACH VET (AI risk prediction) | Moderately known | Evaluation shows poor death prediction | A **cautionary** example. Do not buy AI risk scores expecting fewer deaths; at most use them to prioritise outreach. |
| Planet Youth | Widely known | Iceland time-series only | **Already exists in Vilnius**. The opportunity is national scale-up with an independent evaluation. |

### Gaps

  - Australia's Way Back Support Service (post-attempt aftercare)
  - caring-contacts RCTs
  - barber gatekeeper training (Lions Barber Collective)
  - text-based crisis lines (Shout, 113)
  - Zero Suicide evaluations
  - Friendship Bench adaptations in Europe and the US
  - Finland's newer national programme

## 6. Healthcare workforce shortages, waiting times and rural access

### Takeaway
Four verified items stand out. **Ambient AI scribes** at scale (Kaiser TPMG: about 15,800 physician hours saved across 2.5m visits) are the fastest workforce "multiplier". **Paramedic-led drop-in clinics** in seniors' housing (Canada's CP@clinic, two RCTs) cut ambulance calls by up to 20%. **Drone-delivered defibrillators** (Sweden) beat ambulances in about two-thirds of cases. **Germany's "Landarztquote"** reserves medical-school places for students who commit to 10 years of rural practice, though applications are falling and retention outcomes are years away.

### Cited Findings

#### Ambient AI scribes: The Permanente Medical Group (Kaiser, N. California)
- Oct 2023–Dec 2024: **7,260 physicians used it in about 2.5 million encounters**, saving an estimated **15,791 hours of documentation (1,794 eight-hour workdays)**. "Pajama time" and time per appointment fell significantly (NEJM Catalyst follow-up). — [AMA](https://www.ama-assn.org/practice-management/digital-health/ai-scribes-save-15000-hours-and-restore-human-side-medicine); [Becker's](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/healthcare-information-technology/ai/16k-hours-saved-ambient-ai-scribes-at-kaiser-permanente/)
- The 10-week pilot covered **3,442 physicians and 303,266 encounters**. — [AMA](https://www.ama-assn.org/practice-management/digital/ai-scribe-saves-doctors-hour-keyboard-every-day)
- **84% of physicians reported a positive experience**, and **47% of patients said their physician looked at the computer less**. — [Permanente](https://permanente.org/analysis-ai-scribes-save-physicians-time-improve-patient-interactions-and-work-satisfaction/)

#### Sweden: Everdrone AED drone delivery (Region Västra Götaland / Karolinska Institutet)
- **2020 pilot:** drones were dispatched to 12 of 53 suspected cardiac-arrest alerts and delivered in 11. They **arrived before the ambulance in 7 (64%), with a median 1 min 52 s head start**. — [Healthcare in Europe](https://healthcare-in-europe.com/en/news/aed-drone-delivery-shows-great-potential.html); [DroneDJ](https://dronedj.com/2020/05/15/everdrone-delivers-defibrillators-by-drone-to-swedish-residents/)
- **2021–22 study (Lancet Digital Health, 2023; 13 months, Gothenburg area):** drones arrived first in **about 67% of cases, about 3 minutes (3:14) earlier on average**. In actual cardiac arrests, the **drone-delivered AED was used in a majority of cases**. — [Karolinska Institutet news](https://news.ki.se/drones-enabled-the-use-of-defibrillators-before-ambulance-arrival); [DroneDJ 2023](https://dronedj.com/2023/11/27/the-lancet-touts-everdrones-effectiveness-in-aed-drone-response-to-cardiac-emergencies/)

#### Canada: Community Paramedicine at Clinic (CP@clinic, McMaster University)
- Trained paramedics run **weekly drop-in health sessions in subsidised seniors' housing** (blood pressure, diabetes and falls risk, plus linkage to the family doctor). — [McMaster](https://news.mcmaster.ca/clinics-by-paramedics-improve-care-cut-911-calls/)
- **Two RCTs found up to 20% fewer 911 calls.** — [McMaster](https://news.mcmaster.ca/clinics-by-paramedics-improve-care-cut-911-calls/); [CMAJ 2018 RCT](https://www.cmaj.ca/content/190/21/E638)
- The pilot recorded a **25% drop in ambulance calls**. — [CMAJ 2018 RCT](https://www.cmaj.ca/content/190/21/E638)
- The cost-effectiveness sensitivity analysis estimated a reduction of **0.88 calls/month/100 apartment units in Hamilton and 0.90 in five other communities**. — [PMC7592288](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7592288)
- CP@clinic now operates in **about 35% of Ontario paramedic services**, and **Health Canada funded national scale-up in 2019**. It is also associated with better quality of life, lower chronic-disease risk and better primary-care linkage. — [McMaster](https://news.mcmaster.ca/clinics-by-paramedics-improve-care-cut-911-calls/)

#### Germany: Landarztquote (rural-GP admission quota, state-level, from about 2020)
- **Bavaria** reserves up to **5.8%** of medical places. One cycle admitted **124 students via the rural quota plus 21 via a public-health-service quota (426 applications)**, and the first round drew 685 applicants. — [Sonntagsblatt/epd](https://www.sonntagsblatt.de/artikel/epd/124-medizinstudienplaetze-ueber-landarztquote-vergeben); [Abendzeitung](https://www.abendzeitung-muenchen.de/bayern/studienplaetze-fuer-landaerzte-114-bewerber-erfolgreich-art-615299,PRINT)
- **Lower Saxony:** applications fell from **299 to 278 to 204 for 60 places**. Students who drop the commitment face a **contract penalty of up to €250,000**. — [Apotheke Adhoc](https://www.apotheke-adhoc.de/nachrichten/detail/panorama/weniger-bewerber-fuer-landarzt-studium/)
- **Saxony-Anhalt** started in 2020 and the share has grown to **6.3%** of places. After one year, **all 61 quota students were still enrolled**. Selection weights an aptitude test at 50%, vocational experience (e.g., as a paramedic or nurse) at 40% and school grade at only 10%. — [Ärzteblatt (1-year review)](https://www.aerzteblatt.de/nachrichten/121187/Sachsen-Anhalt-zieht-ein-Jahr-nach-Start-der-Landarztquote-positives-Fazit); [Ärzteblatt (130+ doctors)](https://www.aerzteblatt.de/news/sachsen-anhalt-mehr-als-130-mediziner-fur-landliche-regionen-d6b4ebcf-9226-4678-807f-996ee39ae7fb)
- **Hesse** requires **10 years' practice in an underserved or at-risk region** after specialist training. — [FIM portal (Hesse)](https://fimportal.de/api/v0/xzufi-services/L100001/389225281/en/pdf)

### Inferences
| Solution | How known | Evidence | Lithuania fit / analogue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ambient AI scribes | Widely known in the US, moderately known in the EU | Large-scale observational | High potential for Lithuanian GPs and specialists. The constraint is Lithuanian-language speech recognition and medical-term quality, plus integration with ESPBI IS (the national e-health system) and EU AI Act/GDPR compliance. Pilot with a vendor that supports Lithuanian and measure time saved. |
| CP@clinic | Little-known outside Canada | 2 RCTs | **High fit and cheap.** Lithuanian ambulance services (GMP) or community nurses could hold weekly sessions in social housing and senior centres, cutting avoidable ambulance calls. |
| Everdrone AED drones | Moderately known | Prospective studies (non-randomised) | Lithuania's flat terrain and dispersed villages suit drones. Needs aviation (TKA) approvals and a dispatch link to the 112 centre. |
| Landarztquote | Little-known outside Germany | Admissions data only (no retention data yet) | Could be paired with Lithuanian state-funded study places and existing rural-doctor incentives. The German experience shows **falling applicant numbers** and long lags; the first quota graduates enter practice only around 2030+. |

### Gaps

  - England's Pharmacy First consultation volumes
  - patient-initiated follow-up (PIFU)
  - Portugal's SIGIC voucher system for waiting-list breaches
  - Finland's Mielenterveystalo national iCBT
  - France's Médicobus and the new GP "docteurs juniors" year in underserved areas
