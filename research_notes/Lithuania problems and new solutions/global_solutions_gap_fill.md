## Q1. Alcohol harm: which pricing, offender-monitoring and labelling tools have measured results?

### Takeaway
Two tools have real outcome evidence. **Minimum unit pricing (MUP)** has an observational evaluation from Scotland: a 13.4% fall in deaths wholly attributable to alcohol, about 156 deaths averted a year, with the largest gains in deprived areas. A second jurisdiction (Wales) has now renewed it, and both are uprating to 65p. **Swift-certain-modest sanction sobriety monitoring** (South Dakota's 24/7 Sobriety) is linked to lower mortality, but effect sizes vary widely by model. Ireland's cancer warning labels, the most-cited labelling "innovation", have been **postponed to September 2028** under trade pressure, so there are no outcome data. Estonia's 2019 excise cut is a cautionary example for Lithuania.

### Cited Findings

#### Scotland: minimum unit pricing (MUP), 50p from May 2018, uprated to 65p in September 2024
- **What and how:** a legal floor price per unit (10 ml) of alcohol. It hits the cheapest, strongest products hardest.
- **Results (final Public Health Scotland evaluation, with the University of Glasgow, published March 2023):**
  - deaths wholly attributable to alcohol fell an estimated **13.4%** up to the end of 2020;
  - wholly attributable hospital admissions fell **4.1%**.
  
  Sources: [Public Health Scotland](https://publichealthscotland.scot/news/2023/march/report-highlights-the-impact-of-minimum-unit-pricing-mup-on-deaths-and-hospital-admissions/) and [British Liver Trust](https://britishlivertrust.org.uk/scottish-minimum-unit-pricing-evaluation-shows-13-4-reduction-in-deaths/).
- **Deaths averted:** about **156 per year** — [Scottish Government](https://www.gov.scot/news/minimum-unit-pricing-reducing-alcohol-deaths/). A British Liver Trust summary gives "about 150" — [British Liver Trust](https://britishlivertrust.org.uk/scottish-minimum-unit-pricing-evaluation-shows-13-4-reduction-in-deaths/).
- **Who benefited most:** the largest reductions were among men and people in the 40% most deprived areas. Researchers noted that "wholly attributable" outcomes capture only part of the likely health effect — [Movendi](https://movendi.ngo/?p=56011).
- **Consumption:** an earlier PHS report found alcohol sales fell about 3% after MUP — [Scottish Government](https://www.gov.scot/news/minimum-unit-pricing-reducing-alcohol-deaths/).
- **Evidence type:** observational. It compares Scotland with England, which has no MUP — [Drink and Drugs News](https://www.drinkanddrugsnews.com/mup-cut-alcohol-deaths-by-13-per-cent-claims-final-report/).
- **Uprating:**
  - Parliament approved the rise in April 2024 — [Drink and Drugs News](https://drinkanddrugsnews.com/scottish-government-set-to-increase-mup-to-65p).
  - The price rose by 15p to **65p from 30 September 2024**, to account for inflation — [Scottish Government](https://www.gov.scot/news/minimum-unit-pricing-rise-1/); [Scottish Government](https://www.gov.scot/news/minimum-unit-price-rises/).
  - Reported shelf effects: the cheapest bottle of wine rose from £4.69 to £6.09, and the cheapest standard blended whisky from £14 to £18.20 — [Scottish Daily Mail via PressReader](https://www.pressreader.com/uk/scottish-daily-mail/20240418/281779929172302).
- **Cost to government:** none was reported in the sources found. MUP is a regulation, not a spending programme (see Inferences).

#### Wales: MUP (50p from March 2020), renewed and rising to 65p from 1 October 2026
- **Final independent evaluation (15 January 2025):** implementation was "smooth and effective", retailer compliance was high, and consumption may have fallen — [Welsh Government written statement](https://www.gov.wales/written-statement-independent-evaluations-minimum-unit-pricing-alcohol).
- **Main visible effect:** fewer of the cheapest, strongest drinks on sale, such as white ciders — [Alcohol Change UK](https://alcoholchange.org.uk/blog/latest-welsh-research-shows-minimum-pricing-remains-a-cornerstone-of-alcohol-harm-reduction).
- **Sunset clause:** the Act was due to expire on 1 March 2026 unless renewed — [Welsh Government operation and effect report 2020–2025](https://www.gov.wales/sites/default/files/publications/2025-07/operation-effect-minimum-pricing-alcohol-provisions-2020-2025.pdf).
- **Renewal:** in February 2026 the Senedd voted to continue MUP and raise it to **65p from 1 October 2026** — [Senedd Research](https://research.senedd.wales/research-articles/last-orders-for-cheap-alcohol-minimum-unit-price-to-rise-to-65p/). No source confirming that the 1 October 2026 rise took effect was found.

#### South Dakota (USA): 24/7 Sobriety (twice-daily breath testing with swift, certain, modest sanctions)
- **How it works:** people arrested or convicted for alcohol-involved offences, such as DUI, must abstain. They are tested frequently, and a failed test brings an immediate short sanction — [RAND press release 2016](https://www.rand.org/news/press/2016/02/09.html).
- **2016 county-level study (Lancet Psychiatry):** county adoption was associated with a **4% drop in mortality**. The link was strongest for causes tied to heavy drinking, such as circulatory conditions. The authors were surprised by the size of the effect and called for more research — [RAND](https://www.rand.org/news/press/2016/02/09.html).
- **2023 individual-level study (JAMA Psychiatry):** covered DUI arrestees in South Dakota from 2004 to 2011 (11,827 participants and 48,834 non-participants).
  - Participation was associated with **about a 50% lower risk of death** over the study period.
  - The most conservative (naive Cox) model gave a hazard ratio of only **0.88 (95% CI 0.77–1.00)**, which is borderline significant.
  
  Sources: [RAND EP70008](https://www.rand.org/pubs/external_publications/EP70008.html); [HealthDay](https://www.healthday.com/healthpro-news/general-health/physician-s-briefing-alcohol-2659482691.html).

#### England and Wales: Alcohol Abstinence Monitoring Requirement (AAMR, "sobriety tags")
- **How it works:** under the Sentencing Act 2020, courts can order adults whose offending is alcohol-related to abstain (or cut down) for up to 120 days. A transdermal ankle tag monitors compliance — [Sentencing Academy effectiveness bulletin 2025](https://www.sentencingacademy.org.uk/effectiveness-bulletin/alcohol-abstinence-monitoring-requirement-aamr/).
- **Pilot results:** the South London pilot (from July 2014) had a **92% compliance rate** — [MOPAC decision](https://www.london.gov.uk/what-we-do/mayors-office-policing-and-crime-mopac/governance-and-decision-making/mopac-decisions--40); [Mayor of London press release](https://www.london.gov.uk/press-releases/mayoral/crackdown-against-alcohol-related-crime).
- **First year of the London roll-out:** 92% of clients were fully compliant, according to the tag vendor — [SCRAM Systems](https://www.scramsystems.com/?p=786).
- **Evidence limits:** evidence so far measures compliance, with "promising results". Evidence on **reoffending is limited and mixed** and comes mostly from pilot sites, not the national roll-out — [Sentencing Academy](https://www.sentencingacademy.org.uk/effectiveness-bulletin/alcohol-abstinence-monitoring-requirement-aamr/).

#### Ireland: mandatory alcohol health-warning labels (Public Health (Alcohol) Act 2018): postponed
- **What:** labels warning about liver disease, alcohol-related cancers and drinking in pregnancy. They were due in **May 2026** — [Irish Examiner](https://www.irishexaminer.com/news/politics/arid-+41673959.html); [Decanter](https://www.decanter.com/wine-news/ireland-delays-landmark-health-warnings-on-alcohol-561722).
- **Postponement:**
  - In July 2025 the government postponed the labels to **September 2028** — [Arthur Cox](https://www.arthurcox.com/knowledge/alcohol-labelling-in-ireland-timeline-for-compliance-deferred/); [The Drinks Business](https://www.thedrinksbusiness.com/2025/07/ireland-delays-alcohol-warning-labels-until-2028/).
  - A search extract gave the precise date as 3 September 2028 under S.I. 423/2025. This was not confirmed in the statute.
- **Reasons given:** trade concerns, and the Health Minister cited "economic circumstances" — [Irish Examiner](https://www.irishexaminer.com/news/politics/arid-+41673959.html).
- **Reactions:** Alcohol Action Ireland criticised the delay, while Drinks Ireland called it "much-needed relief" — [Just Drinks](https://www.just-drinks.com/news/trade-bodies-welcome-ireland-alcohol-health-warning-labels-delay/).
- **Evidence:** no outcome data exist, because the labels are not yet in force.

#### Estonia and Finland: lessons from excise cuts (cautionary)
- **Estonia's 2019 cut:** Estonia cut excise on beer, cider and spirits by **25% from July 2019** to curb cross-border buying from Latvia — [Movendi](https://movendi.ngo/policy-updates/2019/06/15/estonia-parliament-adopts-alcohol-tax-reduction/).
- **Consumption before the cut:** 10.1 L of pure alcohol per capita in 2018, the lowest in a decade, with earlier tax rises credited for part of the fall — [Movendi](https://movendi.ngo/policy-updates/2019/06/15/estonia-parliament-adopts-alcohol-tax-reduction/).
- **WHO/European Observatory 2023 review:**
  - The tax cut and COVID-19 were both clearly visible in consumption and health data.
  - The target of under 8 L per capita was **not achieved**.
  - Youth drinking and intoxication fell notably.
  - The review recommended raising excise again.
  
  Source: [European Observatory HSPM](https://eurohealthobservatory.who.int/monitors/health-systems-monitor/updates/hspm/hspm-estonia-2023/estonian-alcohol-policy-evaluation-and-policy-considerations).
- **2023:** adult consumption was **10.9 L per person** (−2.7% on 2022), linked to recession and cost of living. Excise revenue still rose 9.1% — [Nordic Alcohol](https://www.nordicalcohol.org/estonia).
- **Finland 2004:** after the tax cut, off-premise vodka prices fell 36%. The literature warns that later tax rises may not bring consumption back down — [PMC2928916](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2928916).

### Inferences
| Solution | Maturity | How known | Evidence strength | Cost | Lithuania adaptation |

Retailers, not the treasury, keep the margin | Lithuania has high excise but (as far as found) no price floor. - The Scottish result is the strongest "new" pricing evidence available since Lithuania's 2017–2018 package. It is also aimed at the same groups (deprived men) who carry Lithuania's alcohol mortality.
- 24/7-style monitoring targets the few heavy drinkers who cause much harm. That suits Lithuania, where general availability is already restricted.

- The EU-law status of MUP (the CJEU/UK Supreme Court Scotch Whisky litigation, 2015–2017) was not re-verified.
- No 2024–2026 Estonian or Finnish consumption data were retrieved. Finland's 2024 change allowing stronger drinks in grocery stores was not researched.
- No digital brief-intervention RCTs (e.g., UCL "Drink Less") were checked, for lack of budget.
- Whether Lithuania has ever formally considered MUP was not checked.

## Q2. Youth bullying: what is the newest evidence on whole-school and cyberbullying programmes?

### Takeaway
**KiVa** now has a large UK cluster RCT (2024): 118 schools, more than 11,000 pupils, with a significant drop in victimisation. A Chilean RCT, however, found only **small** effects, and the online-game component added nothing. That suggests transfer is possible but effects shrink outside Finland. Denmark's **Fri for Mobberi** (ages 0–8) is popular but has only self-reported evaluation. Sweden's state evaluation found some programmes, including Friends' peer "buddy" model, could **increase** bullying. Cyberbullying programmes have small average effects (g ≈ −0.13 to −0.18 in a 50-study review). Lithuania already runs Olweus (OPKUS) at scale, in 466 schools since 2008.

### Cited Findings

#### KiVa in the UK: "Stand Together" multicentre cluster RCT (Bowes et al., Psychological Medicine, 2024)
- **Design:** a two-arm pragmatic cluster RCT with an embedded economic evaluation, followed up at 12 months.
  - Scale: **118 primary schools** in England and Wales; **11,111 pupils** completed bullying surveys, and teachers reported on 11,571 pupils.
  - Pre-registered target: a 4-percentage-point absolute (22% relative) reduction in self-reported victimisation.
  
  Sources: [ISRCTN12300853](https://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN12300853); [Cardiff ORCA](https://orca.cardiff.ac.uk/id/eprint/176535/).
- **Result:** a "significant reduction in victimization in favour of KiVa" — [University of Turku press release](https://www.utu.fi/en/news/press-release/large-trial-in-united-kingdom-indicates-kiva-antibullying-programme-effectively); [Oxford Dept of Experimental Psychology](https://www.psy.ox.ac.uk/publications/publication_modal/2035616).
- **Citation:** Bowes L. et al. (2024), Psychological Medicine 54(15):4362–4373, DOI 10.1017/S0033291724002666. A corrigendum fixed only an author-name spelling — [Cambridge corrigendum](https://resolve.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/A9A22B0E8D1B942BE0E76CD6ED576DCF/S0033291725000248a.pdf/the-effects-and-costs-of-an-anti-bullying-program-kiva-in-uk-primary-schools-a-multicenter-cluster-randomized-controlled-trial-corrigendum.pdf).
- **Discrepancy:** the 2022 protocol planned 116 schools and about 13,000 pupils aged 7–11 in North Wales, the West Midlands, South East and South West England — [BMC Public Health protocol via Warwick WRAP](https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/164226). The final paper reports 118 schools.
- **Cost-effectiveness:** a separate cost-effectiveness analysis exists, "The long life-time shadow of bullying: cost-effectiveness of KiVa" — [IJPDS](https://ijpds.org/index.php/ijpds/article/view/1745). Its figures were not retrieved.

#### KiVa in Chile: three-arm cluster RCT (socially vulnerable schools in Santiago; grades 5–6, ages 10–12)
- **Design:** 39 schools (13 per arm), with 5,923 pupils at baseline and 3,968 at endpoint. Registered as NCT02898324.
- **Results:**
  - The "partial KiVa" arm (without the online game) reduced victimisation and witnessing of bullying.
  - **Full KiVa with the online game had no effect** on victimisation.
  - No arm changed perpetration.
  - The authors concluded the effects were "small".
  
  Sources: [PMC9528289](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9528289/); [Universidad de los Andes](https://investigadores.uandes.cl/en/publications/effectiveness-of-the-kiva-antibullying-program-with-and-without-t-2/). Published in European Psychiatry (2021) and Prevention Science (2022).

#### Denmark: Fri for Mobberi ("Free of Bullying"), Mary Foundation and Save the Children Denmark
- **What:** an early-years programme for daycare, nurseries and the lower grades of primary school. Material targets ages 0–8 (one municipal document says 3–8).
  - Launch year conflicts: 2007 vs 2008.
  - It expanded to nurseries and childminders around 2021, when an alliance of Mary Fonden, Red Barnet and Børns Vilkår was formed.
  
  Sources: [Esbjerg municipality agenda](https://arkiv.dagsordener.esbjerg.dk/pdf/GetDagsorden/a32dafc7-28f9-4085-8d69-a326bb1efc38); [Silkeborg daycare material](https://soehoejlandet.silkeborg.dk/p/Subsites/Daginstitutioner/Soehoejlandet/Fri%20For%20Mobberi/Fri%20for%20mobberi.pdf); [Børns Vilkår](https://bornsvilkar.dk/?p=3811); [Altinget](https://www.altinget.dk/artikel/ny-alliance-skal-saette-en-stopper-for-mobning).
- **Evidence:** a 2012 evaluation surveyed school and daycare leaders.
  - Leaders rated the materials positively, and daycare leaders reported "signs" of more inclusive peer groups.
  - Almost all institutions said they use the materials.
  - **This is self-report, not measured bullying outcomes** — [Esbjerg agenda document](https://arkiv.dagsordener.esbjerg.dk/pdf/GetDagsorden/a32dafc7-28f9-4085-8d69-a326bb1efc38).

#### Sweden: national evaluation of anti-bullying programmes, including Friends (cautionary)
- **Design:** Skolverket (the National Agency for Education) evaluated eight programmes in 39 schools: Farsta, Friends, Lions Quest, Olweus, SET, Skolkomet, School Mediation and Second Step. It followed individuals over three occasions. The design was observational, not randomised — [Skolverket report](https://www.skolverket.se/download/18.6bfaca41169863e6a659f2c/1553964517627/pdf2849.pdf).
- **Findings:**
  - Some methods were ineffective, and some programmes "resulted in more bullying".
  - Friends' peer "buddy support" model was criticised because the buddies became targets. Friends later revised the programme.
  
  Source: [Sveriges Radio](https://www.sverigesradio.se/artikel/5237115).
- **Programme contamination:** schools often ran several programmes at once, which complicated attribution (Flygare et al., 2013, American Journal of Evaluation) — [Örebro University](https://oru.se/english/employee/erik_flygare).

#### Cyberbullying prevention: meta-analytic evidence
- **NIJ-funded review:** 50 studies, 320 effect sizes, 45,371 participants. Programmes reduced cyberbullying **perpetration (g = −0.18)** and **victimisation (g = −0.13)**, which are small effects — [NIJ](https://nij.ojp.gov/library/publications/systematic-review-and-meta-analysis-interventions-decrease-cyberbullying).
- **2025 meta-analysis of adolescents (Aggression and Violent Behavior):** 30 studies.
  - Victimisation fell (g = −0.331) and perpetration fell (g = −0.454).
  - Effects were larger in samples with more than 50% girls and in quasi-experimental designs, which hints at design bias.
  - Longer programmes improved perpetration outcomes.
  
  Source: [Hacettepe University record](https://research.hacettepe.edu.tr/en/publications/addressing-cyberbullying-in-adolescents-a-comprehensive-meta-anal/).
- **2023 perpetration meta-analysis:** 9 studies, 17 interventions. It found "hardly any conclusive results" in the literature — [Revista Fuentes](https://revistascientificas.us.es/index.php/fuentes/article/view/21525).

#### Lithuania baseline (what already exists)
- The Olweus Bullying Prevention Program (OPKUS) has run since 2008 and has been **implemented in 466 schools**. About one-third still take part in the Olweus Quality Assurance System — [Lituanistika](https://www.lituanistika.lt/content/108097).
- A 2022 Lithuania–Norway comparison surveyed **1,576 teachers in 99 Lithuanian OBPP schools** — [Nordic Studies in Education via DOAJ](https://doaj.org/article/9e463bfbeb6943bfa84734a3a36eadb3).
- A WHO HBSC report (2020) found that almost a third of Lithuanian adolescents experience bullying, one of the highest rates in Europe. The rate peaks at age 13 (32% of boys, 31% of girls) — [LRT](https://www.lrt.lt/en/news-in-english/19/1180226/lithuanian-adolescents-among-most-bullied-in-europe-who-survey).

### Inferences
| Solution | Maturity | How known | Evidence strength | Lithuania adaptation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| KiVa (UK RCT 2024) | Mature, licensed, in many countries | Widely known | RCT (large) | Lithuania already has OPKUS in 466 schools, so the case for KiVa should rest on a **head-to-head randomised comparison** in schools where Olweus has lapsed (about two-thirds). KiVa's licensing and translation cost was not found. |
| KiVa (Chile RCT) | Same | Moderately known | RCT (small effects) | A warning that effects shrink in new contexts and that the digital game may not help. Fidelity and teacher training are the key levers. |
| Fri for Mobberi (Denmark) | Mature in Denmark | Little-known outside the Nordics | Anecdotal/self-report | A cheap upstream add-on for kindergartens (0–8), before the age-13 peak. Treat as a pilot with measurement. |
| Swedish programme evaluation (Friends) | n/a | Little-known | Evaluation (observational) | Avoid untrained peer-"buddy" or mediation components, and evaluate before scaling. |
| Cyberbullying programmes | Mixed | Moderately known | Meta-analyses (small effects) | Fold online-bullying modules into whole-school programmes. Don't expect stand-alone apps to move HBSC rates. |

- The new evidence points toward **implementation quality in Lithuania's existing Olweus network** (re-certifying the roughly 300 lapsed schools) as a faster, lower-risk move than importing a new brand. KiVa remains the best-tested alternative.

### Gaps
- KiVa UK effect sizes, odds ratios and cost per pupil were not retrieved (WebFetch failed). The full text at DOI 10.1017/S0033291724002666 is needed.
- No new KiVa RCTs from Italy or the Netherlands (2023–2025) were retrieved, for lack of budget.
- The Lithuania HBSC 2021/22 victimisation rate was not retrieved.
- The outcomes of the OPKUS 2008–2019 first-cohort study were not retrieved (the abstract was truncated).
- Whether any Lithuanian schools use KiVa is unknown; nothing was found.
- No Danish outcome study of Fri for Mobberi, and no 2023+ Friends trial, was found.

## Q3. Pensioner poverty: which top-ups, automatic-entitlement and senior-work models work?

### Takeaway
The most transferable lessons concern **reaching eligible people automatically**:
- Germany's Grundrente reaches about 1.4 million pensions (6.4% of old-age pensions) with an average top-up of about €92–101 a month. That was still only about half of those assessed as entitled by end-2024.
- Belgian research estimates non-take-up of the main elderly means-tested benefit (GRAPA) at up to 79% and finds automation is "an effective strategy".
- France's 39-territory "zero non-take-up" experiment (€18m) relies on outreach; its evaluation is due in 2026.

Estonia pays a small, targeted annual allowance (about €200 in 2023) to about 96,000 pensioners living alone. Japan's Silver Human Resource Centers give about 700,000 seniors low-paid community work, but membership is falling.

### Cited Findings

#### Germany: Grundrente (basic-pension supplement, from 2021)
- **Reach:** at 31 December 2024, about **1.4 million pensions** carried a Grundrente supplement. That is **5.4% of all pensions and 6.4% of old-age pensions** (Rentenversicherungsbericht 2025) — [Versicherungsbote](https://www.versicherungsbote.de/id/4947000/Grundrente-14-Millionen-Rentner-bekommen-Grundrentenzuschlag/).
- **Average supplement (sources differ by base and date):**
  - **€101/month** for old-age pensions, with an average total payment of €1,024 including the supplement, and €97 across all pension types — [Versicherungsbote](https://www.versicherungsbote.de/id/4947000/Grundrente-14-Millionen-Rentner-bekommen-Grundrentenzuschlag/).
  - About **€92/month** in 2024, according to the Labour Ministry. Per a search extract, which echo24 URL carries this figure is uncertain — [echo24](https://www.echo24.de/welt/mehr-geringverdienende-als-je-zuvor-bekommen-grundrente-gezahlt-eine-gruppe-geht-leer-aus-zr-93860430.html).
  - By sex: men €78.98, women €104.24, citing Bild — [echo24](https://www.echo24.de/verbraucher/grundrente-so-hoch-faellt-zuschlag-frauen-maenner-aus-mehr-anspruch-geringverdiener-geld-berechtigte-93862336.html).
- **Take-up:** the DRV reported about **2.75 million "Anspruchsberechtigte"** at end-2024, and in 2024, for the first time, more than half of them were actually paid — [echo24](https://www.echo24.de/welt/mehr-geringverdienende-als-je-zuvor-bekommen-grundrente-gezahlt-eine-gruppe-geht-leer-aus-zr-93860430.html).
- **Regional pattern:** in Thuringia, Saxony and Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania, 9–10% of old-age pensioners receive it, against 4–7% in western states. Amounts are higher in the West — [echo24](https://www.echo24.de/verbraucher/grundrente-so-hoch-faellt-zuschlag-frauen-maenner-aus-mehr-anspruch-geringverdiener-geld-berechtigte-93862336.html).
- **Statistics:** DRV data on supplements by pension type — [Sozialpolitik aktuell](https://www.sozialpolitik-aktuell.de/files/sozialpolitik-aktuell/_Politikfelder/Alter-Rente/Datensammlung/PDF-Dateien/abbVIII48b.pdf).

#### Estonia: allowance for a pensioner living alone (üksi elava pensionäri toetus)
- **Eligibility:** the person must live alone for the whole period from 1 April to 30 September of the payment year. Their pension must be **below 1.2× the average Estonian old-age pension** — [Sotsiaalkindlustusamet](https://www.sotsiaalkindlustusamet.ee/en/pension-and-benefits/other-pension-related-benefits-and-allowances/allowance-pensioner-living-alone).
- **Amount and reach:** a 2023 Riigikogu explanatory note put the allowance at **€200, paid once a year with the October pension**, to **about 95,830 people** the previous year. The same draft proposed removing the 1.2× cap; whether it passed was not found — [Riigikogu document](https://www.riigikogu.ee/download/3d539d19-b033-4f40-ac1a-e65c3ec1683d).
- **Reference level:** the average old-age pension rises to **€860/month from April 2026**, which shifts the eligibility threshold — [ERR News](https://news.err.ee/1609966040/average-monthly-pension-to-rise-5-3-percent-to-860-from-april).

#### Japan: Silver Human Resource Centers (Shirubā Jinzai Sentā)
- **Membership:** about **698,000 members at the end of FY2020**, down 17,000 on the year, from a peak above 790,000 around FY2009. The decline is linked to firms raising retirement ages and rehiring retirees, plus COVID-19. Per a search extract, which result carried these figures is uncertain — [Between Policy and Practice (NEICON archive)](https://arch.neicon.ru/xmlui/handle/123456789/3570192); [NRI](https://www.nri.com/en/knowledge/report/20170301_2.html).
- **Earnings:** average monthly earnings are typically **under US$400**, as a pension supplement — [Honolulu Star-Advertiser](https://staradvertiser.com/?p=1063246) (attribution per search extract).
- **Health:**
  - A survey of 970 participants found that mental-health scores varied by job type (desk, management and technical roles scored higher) and by motive (social participation scored higher than income) — [Kim & Jeong, JCSW repository](https://jcsw.repo.nii.ac.jp/records/68).
  - A KAKEN project found that falls are common among frail members — [KAKEN 21H03209](https://kaken.nii.ac.jp/grant/KAKENHI-PROJECT-21H03209).
  - Evidence is observational.

#### Belgium: automatic granting of means-tested benefits (GRAPA, the income guarantee for the elderly)
- **Scale of non-take-up:** the pension reform commission estimated GRAPA non-take-up could reach **79%** if every older person below the threshold who does not receive it is counted. The commission considered this likely an overestimate — [University of Antwerp report](https://repository.uantwerpen.be/docstore/d:irua:21911).
- **TAKE project (BRAIN-be, to 2022):** "efforts to automate take-up have proven to be an effective strategy for reducing non-take-up, and this strategy should be strengthened". It also called for better monitoring — [BELSPO TAKE summary](https://www.belspo.be/belspo/brain-be/projects/FinalReports/TAKE_summ_en.pdf).
- **BELMOD (2019–2021):** run by FPS Social Security to support simplification and automatic granting of means-tested benefits, including the elderly income guarantee — [FPS Social Security BELMOD description](https://socialsecurity.belgium.be/sites/default/files/content/docs/fr/elaboration-politique-sociale/belmod/description_du_projet_belmod_eng.pdf).

#### France: Territoires zéro non-recours (TZNR) experiment
- **Set-up:** created by the 3DS law of February 2022 and funded with **€18m over three years**. It covers **39 territories**: 11 started in 2023 and 28 in January 2024 — [Banque des Territoires](https://www.banquedesterritoires.fr/territoires-zero-non-recours-un-decret-precise-les-modalites-de-mise-en-oeuvre-de-lexperimentation); [Ministère des Solidarités](https://solidarites.gouv.fr/lancement-de-lexperimentation-territoires-zero-non-recours).
- **Methods:** outreach ("aller-vers"), including street teams, "rights buses", door-to-door visits and training municipal staff — [Banque des Territoires](https://www.banquedesterritoires.fr/territoires-zero-non-recours-une-mise-en-oeuvre-effective-de-lexperimentation-lechange).
- **Cour des comptes flash audit (30 October 2025):**
  - The experiment and its evaluation were effectively set up.
  - Data-sharing between institutions is a brake.
  - A first evaluation report to Parliament was expected in **June 2026**.
  
  Sources: [Banque des Territoires](https://www.banquedesterritoires.fr/territoires-zero-non-recours-une-mise-en-oeuvre-effective-de-lexperimentation-lechange); [IFRAP](https://www.ifrap.org/emploi-et-politiques-sociales/non-recours-aux-aides-sociales-un-sujet-qui-avance).
- **Baseline:** RSA non-take-up is about **34%** (DREES) — [Ministère des Solidarités](https://solidarites.gouv.fr/lancement-de-lexperimentation-territoires-zero-non-recours).
- **Local example (Bastia, earlier phase):** **1,340 households contacted, 505 interested, 350 new entitlements opened** — [Ville de Bastia press kit](https://www.bastia.corsica/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/20240208_tznrd.pdf).

#### Already covered elsewhere
- St. Gallen's Zeitvorsorge time bank is covered in `global_social_health_innovations.md`, Section 4.

### Inferences

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grundrente | Mature (2021–) | Moderately known | Administrative data; no causal poverty evaluation found | Not found | Lithuania's poverty is concentrated in lone, older women with short or low contribution records. ## Q4. Labour shortages and immigrant integration: which recruitment, retention, recognition and language models show results?

### Takeaway
**Employer- and community-anchored immigration** has the best retention evidence. Canada's Atlantic Immigration Program retained newcomers better than provincial nominee programmes, and rural-pilot communities report 75–100% retention. **Work-first refugee integration** in Denmark has shown employment gains: a related 2013 policy that matched refugees to shortage occupations raised employment by 10 percentage points after two years (NBER). Germany's new tools (Chancenkarte, Anerkennungspartnerschaft) are too new to evaluate: about 11,500 Chancenkarten were issued in about 18 months, and evaluation is due only in 2027. Singapore's legal **right to re-employment to age 69** (from July 2026) is a little-known option for keeping older workers.

### Cited Findings

#### Germany: Chancenkarte (Opportunity Card, points-based job-search residence, since June 2024)
- **Uptake:** **11,479 visas/Chancenkarten** were issued between 1 January 2024 and 15 June 2025, with India the top origin (3,721). A VisaGuard post citing the same government answer gives 11,497. The counts differ slightly, and the period start of 1 January 2024 predates the June 2024 launch; neither point is explained — [Bundestag Drucksache 21/2262](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/022/2102262.pdf); [VisaGuard](https://www.visaguard.berlin/post/chancenkarte-erste-offizielle-zahlen-zeigen-großes-interesse-über-11-000-visa-seit-einführung).
- **Data and evaluation:** sex, age and occupational background are not recorded statistically, and there is little data on jobs found. **Evaluation is planned for 2027.** Three holders in the central register had applied for asylum — [Bundestag Drucksache 21/2262](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/022/2102262.pdf).
- **Rules:** holders may do trial work or part-time work, described as about 20 h/week — [visaright.eu](https://visaright.eu/de/blog/chancenkarte).
- **Assessments:** consultants note a weak data picture and practical hurdles — [EY](https://www.ey.com/de_de/technical/news-zum-internationalen-mitarbeitereinsatz/chancenkarte-top-oder-flop-eine-erste-bestandsaufnahme). The official portal reports high interest — [Make it in Germany](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/de/service/newsletter-juli25).

#### Canada: Atlantic Immigration Program (AIP; pilot from 2017)
- **IRCC evaluation (2017 to 2019–20):** most AIP newcomers were still in Atlantic Canada after their first year. Early evidence showed **higher retention than other economic programmes** in the region — [IRCC evaluation](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/reports-statistics/evaluations/eval-atlantic-immigration-pilot.html).
- **Statistics Canada study (2022, partly by IRCC):** AIP retained immigrants better than provincial nominee programmes one year after admission. Two-year retention was higher in New Brunswick and Newfoundland & Labrador — [CIC News](https://www.cicnews.com/2022/12/more-newcomers-are-choosing-to-settle-long-term-in-atlantic-canada-1232248.html).
- **Regional retention trends (2024 report):** New Brunswick's skilled-worker retention reached 65.8% in 2020. Newfoundland & Labrador rose from 31.3% to 50% between 2016 and 2020 — [CIC News 2024](https://cicnews.com/2024/02/study-more-newcomers-are-choosing-to-stay-in-atlantic-canada-0242996.html).
- **Evaluation presentation:** [CABE/AAAE slides](https://cabe.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/AAAE-event-Short-Term-Retention-Rates-of-Economic-Immigrants-in-Atlantic-Provinces-Evaluation-of-the-Atlantic-Immigration-Program.pdf).

#### Canada: Rural and Northern Immigration Pilot (RNIP), succeeded by the Rural Community Immigration Pilot (RCIP), January 2025
- **How it works:** communities recommend candidates with local job offers.
- **Retention:**
  - In an October 2022 survey, **87%** of RNIP newcomers said they had stayed and planned to stay (self-reported).
  - A December 2025 fact sheet lists measured retention after arrival (2022) by province: ON 92%, MB 96%, SK 100%, AB 75%, BC 82%. It also reports a 91% temporary-to-permanent residence rate. Chart labels were not fully confirmed.
  - **More than 11,000 newcomers** had been admitted by September 2025.
  
  Source: [Northern Policy Institute](https://www.northernpolicy.ca/upload/documents/rcip-fcip-101-magnetic-north-2025.pdf).
- **Thunder Bay:** about 500 employers took part and more than 900 skilled workers were retained (local claim) — [TBNewswatch](https://tbnewswatch.com/local-news/immigration-pilot-program-success-celebrated-9630503).
- **Successor pilots:** Canada launched the Rural and Francophone Community Immigration Pilots in January 2025 — [IRCC news](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/news/2025/01/canada-launches-rural-and-francophone-community-immigration-pilots.html).
- **Evidence:** academic work says rural retention effectiveness "remains understudied" — [EBSCO journal article](https://www.ebsco.com/articles/law/3c22f4e4-96e6-521e-8323-a5ba429448c0/why-should-i-move-to-the-countryside-exploring-new-attempts-for-fostering-inclusion-in-rural-communities).

#### Denmark: IGU (Integrationsgrunduddannelse, integration basic education; tripartite deal, 2016)
- **What:** a two-year programme combining school and paid practical workplace training. It targets refugees and family-reunified people aged 18–40 who have lived in Denmark under 10 years. It began as a 3-year trial in 2016 and has been **extended to 2028** — [European Commission (EWSI)](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/news/denmark-igu-state-integration-project-extended-2024-05-10_en).
- **Results:**
  - An employer-confederation (DA) analysis credited the 2016 tripartite deal, partly through IGU, with refugee employment of about 60% among "job-ready" refugees. This is not an independent evaluation — [ETUC](https://www.etuc.org/refugees-find-work-thanks-tripartite-deal).
  - A 2018 evaluation called IGU a success, but its numbers were not retrieved — [University of Bologna SDGs & Migration](https://www.sdgsmigration.unibo.it/portfolio/evaluation/).
- **Related causal evidence:** a 2013 Danish policy matched refugees to locally short occupations after basic training. It raised employment by **5–6 pp after one year and 10 pp after two years** (Foged, Kreuder & Peri) — [NBER w29781](https://nber.org/papers/w29781).

#### Germany: Anerkennungspartnerschaft (recognition partnership, from 1 March 2024)
- **How it works:** foreign-trained professionals, notably nurses, can enter Germany and start working with an employer while their qualification recognition is processed there, instead of waiting abroad — [Lazarus](https://www.lazarus.at/2024/02/29/zuwanderung-von-auslaendischen-pflegekraeften-nach-deutschland-ab-1-maerz-erleichtert/?print=pdf).
- **Recognition demand (BIBB data via blog):** 2024 saw **about 71,000 recognition applications** (+14% on the year), **22,425 of them for nursing professionals** — [VisaGuard](https://www.visaguard.berlin/post/anerkennung-auslandischer-berufsabschlusse-erreicht-neuen-hochststand).
- **Third-country nurses employed:** 61,548 (2017) → **145,915 (2022)** — [FES](https://library.fes.de/pdf-files/a-p-b/20727.pdf).
- **Official portal:** [Anerkennung in Deutschland](https://www.anerkennung-in-deutschland.de/html/de/ag/10536.php).

#### Finland: Talent Boost (national programme, 2017/2018–)
- **2020 impact brief:** the basic infrastructure was built, but activities needed scaling. It proposed a monitoring model — [Business Finland](https://businessfinland.fi/4a7092/globalassets/julkaisut/talent-boost-impact-brief.pdf).
- **University projects:** Aalto's 2021–2024 project included Finnish and Swedish teaching for international experts and employment support for international students — [Aalto University](https://www.aalto.fi/en/news/talent-boost-strategy-project-at-aalto-in-2021-2024); [Metropolia](https://www.metropolia.fi/en/about-us/talent-boost-programme).
- **Evidence:** no outcome evaluation of employment or retention was found.

#### Sweden and Finland: language training and employment
- **Sweden (register study, Khaef 2026):** immigrants who attend Swedish for Immigrants (SFI) reach their first job faster than comparable non-attendees (inverse-probability weighting). The gain is largest in metropolitan areas — [Population Europe](https://www.population-europe.eu/node/21245) (attribution per search extract).
- **Gothenburg RCT of early intensive intervention for refugees:** SFI course completion rose sharply in the treatment group. Whether language gains drive employment effects is contested. The paper cites Finnish evidence (Sarvimäki & Hämäläinen 2016) of a **large positive earnings effect** of language-focused integration plans — [arXiv 2203.00487](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.00487).
- **Evaluation history:** in 2008 Sweden's National Audit Office said SFI had gone 40 years without an effect evaluation — [The Local](https://www.thelocal.se/20080619/12538/).

#### Singapore: statutory re-employment for older workers
- **Rules:** from **1 July 2026** the retirement age is 64 and the **re-employment age 69**. Employers must offer re-employment to eligible staff (citizens or permanent residents, with satisfactory performance and medical fitness). The target is 65/70 by 2030 — [Nexdigm](https://www.nexdigm.com/insights_post/singapore-retirement-and-re-employment-age-2026/); [MustShareNews](https://mustsharenews.com/retirement-age-raised/).
- **Employment:** the 65–69 employment rate was **41.5% (2016)** — [Ministry of Manpower 2018 speech](https://www.mom.gov.sg/newsroom/parliament-questions-and-replies/2018/0206-speech-by-mr-sam-tan-minister-of-state-for-manpower-in-reply-to-the-motion-on-support-for-seniors-moved-by-mp-henry-kwek).
- **Recent outcomes (per search extracts; per-URL attribution uncertain):**
  - The 55–64 employment rate rose from 66.8% to 70%.
  - More than 9 in 10 eligible senior workers who wanted to continue were offered re-employment in 2023.
  
  Sources: [AsiaNews](https://www.asianews.it/en/south-east-asia/singapore/singapore-raises-retirement-age-to-64-and-incentivises-re-employment-at-an-older-age); [HRD Asia](https://www.hcamag.com/asia/news/general/tripartite-support-for-hrds-in-an-ageing-workforce/143342).

### Inferences
| Solution | Maturity | How known | Evidence | Lithuania adaptation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Canada AIP | Mature (pilot 2017; permanent since about 2022, background knowledge, unverified) | Moderately known | Government evaluation plus StatCan retention comparison | Most relevant model for keeping workers outside Vilnius and Kaunas. Combine designated employers, a guaranteed job offer and a settlement plan per worker (mechanism from background knowledge, unverified), e.g., for Central Asian workers in regional logistics and manufacturing. |
| Canada RNIP/RCIP | Pilot → new pilot (2025) | Little-known | Partial evaluation; self-reported and short-run retention | Let municipalities (savivaldybės) recommend candidates for local shortage jobs, linked to a fast-track residence permit. |
| Denmark IGU (plus 2013 job-matching) | Mature (2016–2028) | Little-known | Evaluation plus NBER causal evidence for a related policy | A 2-year paid "work + Lithuanian language" track for temporary-protection Ukrainians and refugees, agreed with social partners (trišalė taryba, the tripartite council). It fits the new Lithuanian-language requirements. |
| Germany Anerkennungspartnerschaft | New (2024) | Little-known | None yet | Let foreign nurses and doctors work in supervised posts while recognition is pending. This addresses Lithuania's health-worker shortages. |
| Germany Chancenkarte | New (2024) | Moderately known | Descriptive only; low volume | Low priority: modest uptake even in a large labour market, and no outcome data until 2027. |
| Finland Talent Boost | Mature | Moderately known | Weak (process evaluation) | Lithuania's "Work in Lithuania" agency is a functional analogue (background knowledge, unverified). Prioritise measurable retention targets. |
| Language-first training (SFI, Finland) | Mature | Widely known | Register study plus RCT | Link free, workplace-scheduled Lithuanian courses to the language requirement, with employer time-off incentives. |
| Singapore re-employment right | Mature, uprated 2026 | Moderately known | Descriptive | A legal duty to offer adjusted contracts to workers past pension age (64–65 in Lithuania). This taps the 65+ group, many of whom are poor (link to Q3). |

## Q5. Rural mobility: what replaces or supplements shrinking rural bus networks?

### Takeaway
Low-cost **community solutions** have mixed evidence:
- **Ride-share benches (Mitfahrbänke):** the one evaluation found high approval but **low actual use**.
- **Volunteer-run Bürgerbusse:** these scale well; NRW has about 150 associations and about 3,200 volunteer drivers.
- **Austrian micro public transport (Mikro-ÖV):** available in about a third of municipalities (684 of 2,093), but several schemes closed when funding stopped.

**Level-4 autonomous rural shuttles** in Japan are operating (Eiheiji 2023; Komatsu carried more than 18,000 passengers in a year), driven by driver shortages. **Flat-rate national tickets** (the Deutschlandticket) cut emissions about 2.5 Mt a year, but uptake is city-heavy.

### Cited Findings

#### Germany: Mitfahrbänke (ride-share benches)
- **Evaluation:** a Frankfurt University of Applied Sciences study in Taunusstein, Hesse (December 2018–March 2019), funded by the RMV transport association with Hochschule RheinMain and Fachzentrum Mobilität im ländlichen Raum. It used interviews and surveys and found **high approval but low actual use** — [Frankfurt UAS project page](https://www.frankfurt-university.de/de/hochschule/fachbereich-1/ffin/forschungsgruppen-des-ffin/relut/forschungsprojekte/mitfahrbanke/); [Final report "Erfolgsfaktor Mitfahrbank?!" (Dec 2019)](https://www.frankfurt-university.de/fileadmin/standard/Hochschule/Fachbereich_1/FFin/Neue_Mobilitaet/Veroeffentlichungen/2020/Schlussbericht_Mitfahrbaenke_19-12-09.pdf).
- **Cost and design guidance:** a TU Dresden guide stresses low cost and effort and sets siting criteria — [TU Dresden Leitfaden](https://rim.ekom21.de/schmitten/sdnetrim/UGhVM0hpd2NXNFdFcExjZdA4RIBuhiC0cgEnl6WQ1smbeUQuEUp5mhXeNMgQdJsB/Leitfaden_Mitfahrbank_der_TU_Dresden.pdf).
- **Bundled offer:** a TU Dresden conjoint study measured willingness to pay for a package of bench, Bürgerbus and delivery service — [TU Dresden](https://fis.tu-dresden.de/portal/en/publications/ermittlung-von-zahlungsbereitschaften-fur-ein-integriertes-mobilitats-und-nahversorgungskonzept-im-landlichen-raum(5e46b8ce-0c91-4a4c-b247-d450e9f682bf).html).

#### Germany: Bürgerbus (volunteer-driven community minibuses)
- **NRW scale:** in September 2024 North Rhine-Westphalia had **almost 150 Bürgerbus associations and about 3,200 volunteer drivers**, and the state extended its funding — [Land NRW](https://www.land.nrw/pressemitteilung/land-weitet-foerderung-fuer-buergerbusse-aus). A 2022 release gave about 3,500 drivers in almost 150 associations — [Land NRW 2022](https://www.land.nrw/pressemitteilung/buergerbustag-2022-mobilitaet-durch-ehrenamt).
- **Other states:** NRW, Lower Saxony and Baden-Württemberg provide state funding — [Difu](https://orlis.difu.de/handle/difu/218701). Lower Saxony associations report high ridership, driven mainly by ticket use — [Landtag Niedersachsen](https://landtag-niedersachsen.de/drucksachen/drucksachen_19_05000/04501-05000/19-04511.pdf).
- **National count:** estimates vary widely (about 100, 200 or 500 Bürgerbusse) depending on definitions and year (search extract; multiple sources).

#### Austria: Mikro-ÖV (on-demand micro public transport: call-shared taxis, Rufbus, village buses)
- **Coverage:** Mikro-ÖV services exist in **684 of 2,093 Austrian municipalities** (about a third) — [futurezone](https://futurezone.at/digital-life/bus-haustuer-mikro-oev-rufbus-sammeltaxi-dorfmobil-mobilitaet-laendlicher-raum/402058882).
- **ISTmobil Korneuburg (Lower Austria):** a district-wide shared call-taxi pilot from 2015, with the state funding **46%** and 17 municipalities on a 3-year contract — [Land Niederösterreich](https://www.noe.gv.at/noe/pressAnnouncement/getPdf/54160). The 2024 successor "Bezirk Korneuburg mobil" had 13 municipalities and was planned only until 30 June 2026 — [NÖ Landtag motion](https://noe-landtag.gv.at/fileadmin/gegenstaende/20/09/995/0995_Antrag.pdf).
- **Failures:** regioMobil in south-west Styria closed for financial reasons after six years in 38 municipalities. Marchfeld Mobil ended in March 2024 after five years, when municipalities cut funding — [Der Standard](https://www.derstandard.at/story/3000000312997/mikro-oeffis-machen-ein-leben-ohne-auto-am-land-moeglich).
- **Funding design:** Upper Austria's funding guideline scales subsidies to municipal fiscal capacity and favours efficient projects — [Land Oberösterreich](https://www.land-oberoesterreich.gv.at/233523.htm).

#### Japan: Level-4 autonomous rural shuttles
- **Eiheiji (Fukui):** Japan's first Level-4 approval. A remotely monitored seven-seat EV runs a route of about 2 km in a depopulating area, from May 2023 — [Nippon.com](https://www.nippon.com/es/news/yjj2023052800311/).
- **Komatsu (Ishikawa, TIER IV):** a paid year-round autonomous bus from March 2024 that carried **more than 18,000 passengers by end-February 2025**, with Level-4 certification in March 2025 — [Automotive World](https://www.automotiveworld.com/news-releases/tier-iv-certified-for-level-4-autonomous-bus-service-in-ishikawa-prefecture/).
- **Kyodo survey (late 2023):**
  - **32 of 47 prefectures (68%)** were considering Level-4 buses.
  - **98 of 127 private bus operators** cut or abolished routes in 2023 because of driver shortages.
  
  Source: [Nordot/Kyodo](https://nordot.app/1115112300680495534).

#### Germany: Deutschlandticket (national flat-rate public-transport pass, May 2023–)
- **Third interim government evaluation (infas, data to September 2025):** the ticket cuts CO₂ by about **2.5 Mt per year**, equivalent to about 3% less car use. Subscriber numbers fell in 2025, and demand is highest in big cities — [Clean Energy Wire](https://www.cleanenergywire.org/news/germanys-flat-rate-transport-ticket-working-mobility-and-climate-govt-evaluation).
- **Ariadne first-year analysis:** 6.7 Mt CO₂ saved, with the 2025 price rise likely to roughly halve the effect — [Clean Energy Wire](https://www.cleanenergywire.org/news/flat-rate-train-ticket-reduced-germanys-transport-emissions-5-first-year-analysis).
- **Fraunhofer ISI MobilKULT:** willingness to change mobility habits exists in all regions, including the countryside, but declined over three survey rounds — [Fraunhofer ISI](https://www.isi.fraunhofer.de/en/presse/2024/presseinfo-14-mobilkult-deutschlandticket-nachhaltigkeit.html).

## Q6. Civil defence and civilian preparedness: which public-information, shelter, drill, alert and neighbourhood models are measurable and transferable?

### Takeaway
Strong models combine three things:
1. **Legal duties on building owners plus maintained shelters.** Finland has about 50,500 shelters for about 4.8 million people, convertible within 72 hours. Poland's 2025 law sets a spending floor of at least 0.3% of GDP.
2. **Realistic drills that test whether civilians actually go to shelters.** Taiwan's 2025 Urban Resilience Exercise did this for the first time in transit hubs and malls.
3. **Universal communication.** Sweden mailed 5.2 million brochures in 2024; reach and knowledge rose, but so did worry (39% vs 24% in 2018), and only about 4 in 10 Swedes are actually prepared.

Ukraine's public-private **Air Alert** app (46 million downloads) and Japan's neighbourhood **jishu-bosai soshiki** (covering 85.4% of households) show the digital and community layers. Lithuania's 20 May 2026 Vilnius red alert exposed exactly these layers.

### Cited Findings

#### Lithuania's trigger event (context)
- **20 May 2026:** the military raised a yellow and then a **red air alert in Vilnius county** after a suspected drone approached from Belarus.
  - Schools and kindergartens were told to move children into shelters.
  - Vilnius airport closed and public transport stopped.
  - The President and Prime Minister were taken to shelters.
  - The alert lasted about an hour, and no drone was sighted over Lithuania.
  - AP called it the first such alert sending people to shelters in an EU/NATO capital since 2022.
  
  Sources: [ABC News/AP](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/residents-lithuanias-capital-told-shelter-drone-alarm-underlines-133141160); [Novaya Gazeta Europe](https://novayagazeta.eu/en/articles/2026/05/20/lithuania-tells-vilnius-residents-to-seek-shelter-after-drone-spotted-near-border-en-news); [bne IntelliNews](https://www.intellinews.com/top-lithuanian-officials-take-shelter-after-air-alert-after-suspected-drone-approaches-from-belarus-443966/).
- The "shelters locked" claim was **not found** in these sources (see Gaps).

#### Sweden: "Om krisen eller kriget kommer" (If Crisis or War Comes), 2024 edition
- **Distribution:** **5.2 million copies**, mailed over two weeks from 18 November 2024 — [MCF/MSB press release](https://mcf.mynewsdesk.com/pressreleases/snart-landar-broschyren-om-krisen-eller-kriget-kommer-hos-drygt-fem-miljoner-hushaall-i-sverige-3354652.pdf).
- **Follow-up survey (about 2,000 people, early 2025):**
  - The brochure was well received, many read it, and knowledge of Sweden's preparedness rose.
  - **39% felt worried on receiving it, against 24% for the 2018 mailing.**
  
  Source: [MSB press release](https://msb.mynewsdesk.com/pressreleases/undersoekning-visar-positivt-mottagande-av-broschyren-men-ocksaa-oekad-oro-3375311.pdf).
- **Earlier survey (early 2024, after senior officials' war warnings, before the brochure):** 38% felt more worried, and about a third had started thinking more about home preparedness — [MSB press release](https://msb.mynewsdesk.com/pressreleases/undersoekning-om-allmaenhetens-reaktioner-efter-skarpa-krigsuttalanden-3302687.pdf).
- **Actual preparedness:** "only 4 in 10 Swedes are prepared for crisis," citing MSB statistics; date and method are unclear — [EFN](https://efn.se/bara-4-av-10-svenskar-ar-forberedda-pa-kris).
- **"Kriskompis":** Swedish business press reported a push for everyone to have a "kriskompis" (crisis buddy). This is a headline only; details not retrieved — [Dagens PS](https://www.dagensps.se/foretag/okad-beredskap-nu-ska-alla-ha-en-kriskompis).

#### Finland: comprehensive security, National Defence Courses and the shelter obligation
- **National Defence Courses:** these give civilian and military leaders an overall view of foreign, security and defence policy. Regional courses focus on local preparedness, civil defence, security of supply, communications and transport — [Security Committee (Turvallisuuskomitea)](https://turvallisuuskomitea.fi/en/national-defence-courses/).
  - The residential course is described as about 3.5 weeks for rising leaders from any sector — [ANU National Security College](https://nsc.anu.edu.au/content-centre/article/interview/could-australia-learn-about-preparedness-finland-prepper-nation).
- **72-hour concept:** authorities and NGOs promote household self-sufficiency for about three days (food, water, medicine) — [Defence24](https://defence24.com/geopolitics/finland-building-a-nation-thats-aware-and-prepared).
- **Shelter stock:** Finland has **about 50,500 civil-defence shelters with places for about 4.8 million people** — [Finnish Government / Ministry of the Interior](https://valtioneuvosto.fi/en/-/1410869/finland-has-civil-defence-shelters-for-about-4.8-million-people).
  - About 85% are privately owned.
  - The Rescue Act (379/2011) requires shelters in residential and workplace buildings over 1,200 m² (industrial over 1,500 m²), with inspection at least every 10 years.
  - Shelters used day-to-day as storage or gyms must be convertible within **72 hours**.
  
  Sources: [CZ Defence](https://www.czdefence.com/article/finland-as-one-of-the-most-resilient-companies-in-europe-inspiration-not-only-for-the-czech-republic); [Wikibooks casebook](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Infrastructure_Past,_Present,_and_Future_Casebook/Finnish_Underground); [Malaysia Now](https://www.malaysianow.com/out-there-now/2023/08/30/finland-counted-its-bomb-shelters-and-found-50500-of-them).
- **Maintenance:** housing companies and property owners are responsible — [Yle](https://yle.fi/a/74-20205668).

#### Poland: Civil Protection and Civil Defence Act ("shelter act"), in force 1 January 2025
- **Adoption:** signed by President Duda in December 2024 — [TVN24](https://tvn24.pl/tvn24-news-in-english/poland-s-president-signs-civil-defense-bill-into-law-st8228044); [Notes from Poland](https://notesfrompoland.com/2024/12/19/poland-introduces-landmark-new-civil-defence-law/).
- **Contents:** evacuation, shelters and places of refuge, with an **annual funding floor of at least 0.3% of GDP** — [Notes from Poland](https://notesfrompoland.com/2024/12/19/poland-introduces-landmark-new-civil-defence-law/); [PAP](https://www.pap.pl/en/news/warsaw-approves-civil-defence-initiative-valued-billions).
- **Obligations:** buildings can be designated as shelters by decision. New public and multi-family buildings carry obligations. The government must report annually to Parliament, first by 30 April 2026 — [Schoenherr](https://www.schoenherr.eu/content/poland-civil-defence-regulations-to-influence-private-sector).
- **Funding plans:** **PLN 102.4bn** is planned for 2027–2031 — [Kurs.com.ua](https://kurs.com.ua/en/novost/1482848-polsha-gotue-masshtabne-finansuvannja-civilnogo-zahistu-do-2031-roku). Evacuation plans were drafted in January 2025 — [Euromaidan Press](https://euromaidanpress.com/2025/01/04/poland-drafts-evacuation-plans-amid-rising-security-concerns/).

#### Taiwan: 2025 Urban Resilience Exercise (merged air-defence, mobilisation and critical-infrastructure drill)
- **Format:** Taiwan merged its national mobilisation, air-defence (Wan An) and critical-infrastructure drills into one exercise held alongside Han Kuang — [Ministry of National Defense policy points, Oct 2025](https://www.mnd.gov.tw/en/Publication/126); [Qatar Tribune](https://www.qatar-tribune.com/article/183638/world/taiwan-expands-its-annual-military-drills).
- **Schedule:** 15–18 July 2025, "with warning, by zone, at different times", with a 30-minute drill per region using phone alerts, sirens and broadcasts — [MND air-defence page](https://adma.mnd.gov.tw/uniten/100008/8010); [WION](https://www.wionews.com/world/taiwan-to-merge-war-games-and-urban-survival-tests-in-drill-to-prepare-people-for-a-china-invasion-1751469802174/amp).
- **New elements:**
  - For the first time, shelter practice took place in **crowded indoor venues** (public transit, shopping areas, government buildings).
  - **18 local governments each chose 3 townships to test whether civilians actually entered shelters.**
  - More than 46,000 personnel and over 16,000 vehicles and aircraft took part.
  
  Sources: [MND](https://www.mnd.gov.tw/en/Publication/126); [MND air-defence page](https://adma.mnd.gov.tw/uniten/100008/8010).
- **Observed drill:** in Taipei, traffic stopped and volunteers guided people into shelters — [CNN via KVIA](https://kvia.com/news/2025/07/17/air-raid-drills-across-taiwan-as-island-practices-for-a-war-with-china-it-hopes-will-never-come/).
- **Fines:** TVBS reports fines of up to NT$150,000 for non-compliance; the year was unclear — [TVBS](https://news.tvbs.com.tw/english/2931933).

#### Ukraine: "Air Alert" (Povitryana Tryvoha) app (public–private)
- **Origin:** Ajax Systems and Stfalcon built it in about a day in March 2022, with Ministry of Digital Transformation support and access to the official alert feed. It covered the whole country within about a month — [Stfalcon](https://stfalcon.com/en/blog/post/air-alert-app); [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/90922662/ajax-systems-air-raid-alert-ukraine).
- **Usage:** **46 million downloads and about 6.7 million daily active users**, with alerts for six threat types (company claim, fourth anniversary) — [Ajax Systems](https://ajax.systems/blog/air-alert-fourth-anniversary/). Earlier: 27 million downloads and more than 6.5 million daily users — [Ajax Systems](https://ajax.systems/blog/air-alert-third-anniversary/); more than 24 million downloads (about 77% of the population) in late 2024 — [Euromaidan Press](https://euromaidanpress.com/2024/12/10/ajax-systems-receives-european-excellence-award-2024-as-its-ukraines-air-alert-app-reaches-24-million-downloads-during-war/).
- **Caveat:** these figures are promotional and unaudited.

#### Japan: jishu-bosai soshiki (voluntary neighbourhood disaster-prevention organisations)
- **Scale:** as of 1 April 2024, **1,697 of 1,741 municipalities** had them, with **167,233 organisations**. The coverage rate is rising — [FDMA White Paper on Fire Service 2024 (R6)](https://www.fdma.go.jp/publication/hakusho/r6/chapter4/para2/68753.html). In 2023 there were 166,923 organisations in 1,692 municipalities — [FDMA White Paper 2023 (R5)](https://www.fdma.go.jp/publication/hakusho/r5/chapter4/para2/68427.html).
- **Coverage:** national activity coverage was **85.4% of households** (1 April 2024, citing the white paper). The 2023 figure in the same series is also shown as 85.4%, which needs checking — [Shizuoka Prefecture statistics](https://toukei.pref.shizuoka.jp/mita/documents/07-16.pdf); [Shizuoka (earlier edition)](https://toukei.pref.shizuoka.jp/mita/documents/06-16.pdf).

## Q7. Cross-cutting: which solutions are the most under-the-radar, best-evidenced fits for Lithuania?

### Takeaway
Ranked on evidence strength, low cost and fit with Lithuanian problems, the top transferable candidates are:
1. A Scottish-style **minimum unit price** (observational evidence; 13.4% fewer alcohol-specific deaths).
2. **Automatic or outreach-based take-up** of pensioner top-ups (Germany, Belgium, France).
3. **Drills that test whether shelters open** (Taiwan) plus **owner-maintained shelters with 72-hour conversion** (Finland).
4. **Employer- and community-anchored immigration with settlement plans** (Canada AIP and RNIP).
5. **Paid work-plus-language tracks** (Denmark IGU).

**Re-certifying Lithuania's existing Olweus schools**, with KiVa tested head-to-head, is the low-risk bullying move. For rural transport, community options (benches, Bürgerbus, Mikro-ÖV) are cheap but fragile without stable state co-funding.

### Cited Findings
- MUP: 13.4% fall in wholly attributable deaths and about 156 deaths averted a year, with the largest effects in deprived areas — [Public Health Scotland](https://publichealthscotland.scot/news/2023/march/report-highlights-the-impact-of-minimum-unit-pricing-mup-on-deaths-and-hospital-admissions/); [Scottish Government](https://www.gov.scot/news/minimum-unit-pricing-reducing-alcohol-deaths/); [Movendi](https://movendi.ngo/?p=56011).
- Automation reduces non-take-up — [BELSPO TAKE](https://www.belspo.be/belspo/brain-be/projects/FinalReports/TAKE_summ_en.pdf). Grundrente reached about 1.4m pensions, but only about half of the 2.75m "Anspruchsberechtigte" were paid — [Versicherungsbote](https://www.versicherungsbote.de/id/4947000/Grundrente-14-Millionen-Rentner-bekommen-Grundrentenzuschlag/); [echo24](https://www.echo24.de/welt/mehr-geringverdienende-als-je-zuvor-bekommen-grundrente-gezahlt-eine-gruppe-geht-leer-aus-zr-93860430.html).
- Taiwan tested actual shelter entry in 3 townships in each of 18 local governments — [MND](https://www.mnd.gov.tw/en/Publication/126). Finland has about 50,500 shelters for about 4.8m people — [Finnish Government](https://valtioneuvosto.fi/en/-/1410869/finland-has-civil-defence-shelters-for-about-4.8-million-people).
- AIP retained immigrants better than provincial nominee programmes — [CIC News](https://www.cicnews.com/2022/12/more-newcomers-are-choosing-to-settle-long-term-in-atlantic-canada-1232248.html). Danish shortage-occupation matching gave +10 pp employment at two years — [NBER](https://nber.org/papers/w29781).
- Lithuania's OPKUS is in 466 schools, about one-third still quality-assured — [Lituanistika](https://www.lituanistika.lt/content/108097). KiVa reduced victimisation in a 118-school UK RCT — [ISRCTN](https://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN12300853).
- Austrian Mikro-ÖV schemes closed when municipal funding ended — [Der Standard](https://www.derstandard.at/story/3000000312997/mikro-oeffis-machen-ein-leben-ohne-auto-am-land-moeglich). The one ride-bench evaluation found low use — [Frankfurt UAS](https://www.frankfurt-university.de/de/hochschule/fachbereich-1/ffin/forschungsgruppen-des-ffin/relut/forschungsprojekte/mitfahrbanke/).

### Inferences
- **Most under-the-radar but promising:**
  - 24/7 Sobriety and sobriety tags;
  - France's TZNR outreach;
  - Belgium's automatic-rights research;
  - Denmark's IGU;
  - Germany's Anerkennungspartnerschaft;
  - Singapore's re-employment right;
  - Austria's Mikro-ÖV funding lessons;
