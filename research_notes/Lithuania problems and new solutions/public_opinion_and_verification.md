Method note for the report writer: these notes date from 13 October 2026. All 35 permitted WebSearch calls were used. **WebFetch failed on every attempt.** The errors were DNS "ENOTFOUND" for lrt.lt, intellinews.com and the European Commission representation site, and the egress proxy refused CONNECT to lrt.lt. ## A1. What do the latest polls show as Lithuanians' top problems (prices, healthcare, pensions, security, emigration, corruption, housing, wages)?

### Takeaway
The most recent hard data on the national top problem is Standard Eurobarometer 104 (autumn 2025), not a 2026 survey. It shows **inflation / cost of living as the clear No. 1 problem (39%, up 3 points year on year)**, then **defence and security (26%)** and **Russia's invasion of Ukraine (20%)**. I found no 2025–2026 domestic poll (Vilmorus, Spinter, Baltijos tyrimai, Norstat) that ranks national problems. Inflation has since risen again, to 6.1% in August and 6.7% in September 2026, so cost of living is very likely still the top concern.

### Cited Findings
**Eurobarometer: national problems (Standard EB 104, autumn 2025; Lithuanian national report published December 2025)**
- Inflation is still the most important problem facing Lithuania, named by **39%** of respondents, **3 points more than in autumn 2024**. Next come **defence and security (26%)** and **Russia's invasion of Ukraine (20%)**. These are Lithuanian-language extracts ("svarbiausia problema Lietuvai išlieka infliacija … 39 proc. … 3 p.p. daugiau nei pernai rudenį"; "gynyba ir saugumas (26 proc.) bei Rusijos invazija į Ukrainą (20 proc.)"). — [lithuania.lt: Life satisfaction in Lithuania reaches a historic high](https://lithuania.lt/governance-in-lithuania/life-satisfaction-in-lithuania-reaches-a-historic-high/); [EC Representation in Lithuania, Standard Eurobarometer 104 Lithuania report (PDF)](https://lithuania.representation.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2025-12/Standartinis_Eurobarometras_104_Lietuvos_ataskaita.pdf)
- Lithuanians name inflation, rising prices and the cost of living as their biggest problems, even though they report record life satisfaction. — [tv3.lt Eurobarometras topic page](https://www.tv3.lt/gaires/eurobarometras)
- The EB 104 Lithuania report has separate sections on "most important national problems" (p. 8) and "most important EU problems" (p. 9). The figures for healthcare, pensions, unemployment, immigration and housing **could not be extracted**. — [EB 104 Lithuania report (PDF)](https://lithuania.representation.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2025-12/Standartinis_Eurobarometras_104_Lietuvos_ataskaita.pdf)
- **EU-level priority (EB 104):** **54%** of Lithuanians name defence and security as the EU's most important priority for the next five years, against an EU average of 34%. That is the highest share of any member state ("Lietuva išsiskiria…"). — [EC Representation in Lithuania press release, 22 Dec 2025](https://lithuania.representation.ec.europa.eu/news/eurobarometras-lietuvos-gyventojai-gynyba-ir-sauguma-ivardija-svarbiausiu-es-prioritetu-2025-12-22_lt)
- February 2026 LRT report on the Eurobarometer: **58%** of people in Lithuania view the EU positively (EU average 49%), **55%** want the EU to strengthen its role in defence and security, and Lithuanians are "more optimistic about the future than most Europeans". — [LRT English, 5 Feb 2026](https://www.lrt.lt/en/news-in-english/19/2827155/people-in-lithuania-more-optimistic-about-future-than-most-europeans)
- The latest Standard Eurobarometer (EB 105) was fielded in **March–April 2026**. Its EU-wide enlargement results were published in May 2026. **No Lithuania national report or Lithuanian top-problem figures for EB 105 were found.** — [European Western Balkans, 11 May 2026](https://europeanwesternbalkans.com/2026/05/11/support-for-eu-enlargement-at-53-across-the-union-lowest-in-france/)
- European Parliament-commissioned Eurobarometer, reported by ELTA on about 1 July 2026: **66%** of Lithuanians view the EU's future with hope, **70%** are hopeful about Lithuania's prospects, **57%** say they are more pessimistic about the world, and **77%** see the EU as a "safe haven" amid geopolitical uncertainty. The extract gives no ranking of priority issues. — [LRT, "Apklausa: Lietuvos gyventojai išlieka optimistais, tačiau iš ES tikisi daugiau"](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/lietuvoje/2/2977081/apklausa-lietuvos-gyventojai-islieka-optimistais-taciau-is-es-tikisi-daugiau)
- Youth Eurobarometer (date not shown in extract): rising prices and the cost of living are the biggest worry for young people in both Lithuania and the EU. — [paninfo.lt](https://paninfo.lt/eurobarometras-es-ir-lietuvos-jaunima-labiausiai-neramina-kylancios-kainos/?pdf=127728)
- **Older Eurobarometer context (flag: earlier waves).** In an earlier wave, inflation was the top problem in Lithuania at 40% (EU 38%), 12 points lower than the wave before. The extract did not identify the wave, and the source was likely tv3.lt's "Optimistų gretos mažėja" article. — [tv3.lt](https://www.tv3.lt/naujiena/verslas/optimistu-gretos-mazeja-n304721). In March 2023, Lithuanians were "more concerned about inflation than most Europeans". — [LRT English, 21 Mar 2023](https://www.lrt.lt/en/news-in-english/19/1943153/lithuanians-more-concerned-about-inflation-than-most-europeans-survey). Before the 2024 European Parliament elections, security topped Lithuanian voters' concerns. — [LRT English (2024)](https://www.lrt.lt/en/news-in-english/19/2253155/security-tops-lithuanian-voter-concerns-in-ep-elections-survey)

**Domestic pollsters**
- **Vilmorus is in bankruptcy.** The Vilnius Regional Court opened bankruptcy proceedings against Vilmorus, a polling firm active for more than three decades. The tv3 topic page is dated **28 Aug 2026**. This probably explains why no new Vilmorus "most pressing problems" series turns up for 2026. — [tv3.lt Vilmorus topic page](https://www.tv3.lt/gaires/vilmorus)
- **Corruption (Vilmorus for the Special Investigation Service, STT, "Lietuvos korupcijos žemėlapis 2025"):** residents rank the **justice system (courts, prosecution, bar) as the most corrupt sector (52%)**. — [STT summary of 2025 Corruption Map (docx)](https://www.stt.lt/data/public/uploads/2026/02/2025-lkz-santrauka_vilmorus_lt.docx)
- No 2025–2026 Delfi/Spinter, Baltijos tyrimai/ELTA or LRT/Norstat poll ranking "the most pressing problems" turned up in four targeted Lithuanian-language searches. A tv3 snippet says "residents are most worried about rising prices, and buying things is getting harder", but it named no pollster or date. — [tv3.lt apklausa topic page](https://www.tv3.lt/gaires/apklausa)
- **Older domestic rankings (flag: outdated).** A 2020 LRT/Norstat poll found that **low wages, low pensions and high prices** were the most important social problems. — [LRT Mediateka, LRT apklausa](https://www.lrt.lt/mediateka/irasas/2000124484/zinios-lrt-apklausa-didziausios-socialines-problemos-mazos-algos-ir-pensijos-aukstos-kainos). A Spinter/Delfi poll from about 2014 had **low wages** at about 45% and **emigration** at 11.6%, per search extract; the extract gave no direct URL beyond the tv3 Spinter topic page. — [tv3.lt Spinter topic page](https://www.tv3.lt/gaires/spinter). In April 2024, residents worried about the threat of war and rising prices. — [alkas.lt, 19 Apr 2024](https://alkas.lt/2024/04/19/gyventojai-nerimauja-del-karo-gresmes-ir-kylanciu-kainu)
- In a Baltic poll for insurer BTA (undated), **healthcare** was what residents considered most important when preparing for unforeseen circumstances. Lithuanians and Estonians put family well-being second. — [tv3.lt apklausa topic page](https://www.tv3.lt/gaires/apklausa)

**Objective context that bears on the salience of cost of living**
- National CPI inflation was **6.1% y/y in August 2026**, the highest since August 2023, and **6.7% in September 2026**. Transport and housing/utilities drove the rise. — [Trading Economics, Aug 2026](https://tradingeconomics.com/lithuania/inflation-cpi/news/581880); [Trading Economics, Sep 2026](https://tradingeconomics.com/lithuania/inflation-cpi/news/590410)
- The at-risk-of-poverty rate was **22.6%** in 2025, the highest in the EU (see B1). — [LRT, 31 Mar 2026](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/lietuvoje/2/2884080/skurdo-rizikos-lygis-lietuvoje-pernai-didejo-1-1-punkto)

### Inferences
- EB 104 was fielded in autumn 2025, when inflation was lower. With inflation back above 6% in August–September 2026, the cost of living is very likely to remain, or become even more clearly, the top concern in the autumn 2026 wave.
- Security is unusually salient in Lithuania. It ranks 2nd and 3rd as a national problem (defence 26%, the war in Ukraine 20%), and 54% call it the top EU priority, the highest in the EU. So "prices plus security" is the two-part headline.
- Healthcare, pensions and wages showed up as top concerns in older domestic polls (2014, 2020). Eurobarometer figures for healthcare and pensions exist in the EB 104 report but could not be retrieved, so no current ranking for these can be cited.

### Gaps
- EB 104 Lithuania percentages for health, pensions, the economic situation, unemployment, housing, immigration, energy and the environment as national problems, plus the personal-problem rankings. The PDF could not be fetched.
- Lithuania data from EB 105 (spring 2026). No national report was found in search.
- Exact Eurobarometer sample size and fieldwork dates for Lithuania. Typically about 1,000 face-to-face interviews, but **not verified from source**.
- No 2025–2026 Spinter, Baltijos tyrimai, Norstat or Vilmorus poll ranking national problems was found. A follow-up should go directly to the Delfi, ELTA, LRT and 15min archives, which could not be reached here.
- Housing affordability as a public concern: no poll found.

## A2. Trust in government and institutions, government approval, and satisfaction with democracy

### Takeaway
Lithuanians trust the **police and army most** and the **Seimas and Government least**. Trust in the Government was about **35% in February–March 2026** (Baltijos tyrimai/ELTA). The OECD put high or moderately high trust in the national government at **36%** in late 2025. Disapproval of the Ruginienė cabinet climbed to **39.6%** in May 2026, shortly before it resigned. Lithuanians trust the EU (66%) far more than their own institutions.

### Cited Findings
- **Baltijos tyrimai for ELTA, fieldwork 19 Feb–2 Mar 2026** (published about 15 Mar 2026; 10 institutions tested): police and army most trusted, Seimas and Government least. **Government trust 35%, up from 32% in January**. Trust in the courts was the best in seven years. — [LRT, "Nauja apklausa: mažiausias gyventojų pasitikėjimas Seimu ir Vyriausybe"](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/lietuvoje/2/2868060/nauja-apklausa-maziausias-gyventoju-pasitikejimas-seimu-ir-vyriausybe)
- **Baltijos tyrimai for ELTA, fieldwork 23 Apr–7 May 2026:** same order, with police and army at the top and Seimas and Government at the bottom. Political scientists note that trust tends to decline over a term of office. The extract gave no percentages. — [tv3.lt gyventojų apklausa topic page](https://www.tv3.lt/gaires/gyventoju-apklausa)
- **Spinter tyrimai for Delfi, May 2026:** **39.6%** rated the Government's work negatively, up from **33.5%** in April, a rise of 6 points. Only **2.1%** rated it positively, per extract. — [LRT, "Naujausia apklausa: 6 proc. padaugėjo neigiamai Vyriausybės darbą vertinančių gyventojų"](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/lietuvoje/2/2955026/naujausia-apklausa-6-proc-padaugejo-neigiamai-vyriausybes-darba-vertinanciu-gyventoju)
- **Spinter tyrimai for Delfi, June 2026 (published 8 July 2026):** after the coalition reshuffle, ratings for the LSDP and PM-designate Mindaugas Sinkevičius improved slightly. Opposition **TS-LKD (Homeland Union) led voting intentions**, with the LSDP second. — [LRT, "Apklausa: performavus koaliciją kiek pagerėjo LSDP, Mindaugo Sinkevičiaus vertinimas"](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/lietuvoje/2/2984024/apklausa-performavus-koalicija-kiek-pagerejo-lsdp-mindaugo-sinkeviciaus-vertinimas)
- A later poll (likely August–September 2026; headline only): "in the party table TS-LKD continues to lead; Kukuraitis rated more favourably". — [LRT](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/lietuvoje/2/3025012/apklausa-partiju-lenteleje-toliau-pirmauja-ts-lkd-palankiau-vertinamas-kukuraitis)
- Opposition figures called the Sinkevičius cabinet "the weakest of the three Social Democrat governments". — [LRT Mediateka](https://www.lrt.lt/mediateka/irasas/2000790135/ivertino-sinkeviciaus-komanda-silpniausia-is-triju-socialdemokratu-vyriausybiu)
- **OECD Trust Survey 2025 (fieldwork Sep–Nov 2025; published 2026):** **36%** of Lithuanians report high or moderately high trust in the national government, against an OECD average of 40%. — [OECD Survey on Drivers of Trust in Public Institutions 2026 Results: Lithuania](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-survey-on-drivers-of-trust-in-public-institutions-2026-results_c88c8869-en/lithuania_cc9301b0-en.html)
- **Eurobarometer EB 104 (autumn 2025):** **66%** of Lithuanians trust the EU (EU average 48%). Views on democracy at home are described as "stable and broadly positive", with a **55%** figure whose referent was cut off in the extract. It is probably satisfaction with how democracy works, but that is **unconfirmed**. — [lithuania.lt](https://lithuania.lt/governance-in-lithuania/life-satisfaction-in-lithuania-reaches-a-historic-high/)
- EU-wide in EB 104, average trust in national governments was **33%** (EU institutions 48%). Baltic citizens trust EU institutions more than their own national ones. — [Європейська правда (eurointegration.com.ua), 15 Dec 2025](https://www.eurointegration.com.ua/news/2025/12/15/7227176/)
- Three in four Lithuanians think the euro is beneficial (late 2025 Eurobarometer, per the Bank of Lithuania). — [Bank of Lithuania](https://www.lb.lt/lt/naujienos/lietuviu-palankumas-eurui-vel-soktelejo-aplenkeme-euro-zonos-vidurki)
- **Older (flag):** in a 2023 Vilmorus poll, **9.9%** trusted the Seimas and **17.5%** the Government. — via search extract around [LRT trust coverage](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/lietuvoje/2/2868060/nauja-apklausa-maziausias-gyventoju-pasitikejimas-seimu-ir-vyriausybe). In a GLOBSEC study (undated, likely 2023 or earlier), **32%** were satisfied with how democracy works. — [GLOBSEC](https://www.globsec.org/what-we-do/publications/perceptions-democracy-and-conspiracy-theories-lithuania)

### Inferences
- Trust in the Government (about 32–36% across Baltijos tyrimai and the OECD) is low but much higher than the Vilmorus 2023 readings (17.5%). This most likely reflects different question wording and response scales between pollsters, not a real doubling. Do not compare figures across pollsters.
- Rising disapproval in spring 2026 matches the government crisis: Ruginienė resigned on 23 June 2026 (see B9).
- The gap between trust in the EU and in national institutions (66% vs about 35%) is a recurring Baltic pattern and fits the strong security framing of opinion.

### Gaps
- The exact Seimas trust percentage for 2026 and any September–October 2026 approval figures for the Sinkevičius government were not found.
- Satisfaction with democracy from EB 104 or EB 105 for Lithuania: unconfirmed (the 55% referent is unclear).
- Sample sizes for the Baltijos tyrimai/ELTA and Spinter/Delfi polls were not in the extracts. Both pollsters usually survey about 1,000 adults, but this is **not verified**.

## A3. Attitudes toward immigrants and foreign workers

### Takeaway
The evidence is mixed. An IOM-commissioned survey (November 2024) found attitudes toward migrants **worsening**, with sharp differences by origin: Ukrainians are viewed most favourably and Middle Eastern migrants least. The European Commission's summary of the 2024 survey found **rising acceptance of labour migrants** and high acceptance of foreign students (79%). Headline figures from the 2025 Social Attitudes Survey were not retrievable.

### Cited Findings
- **IOM survey (reported 25 Nov 2024):** attitudes toward migrants worsened. Average favourability: Ukrainians about **7/10**, Belarusians **5/10**, Central Asian labour migrants **4.83/10**, Middle Eastern migrants **4.13/10**. — [LRT English, 25 Nov 2024](https://www.lrt.lt/en/news-in-english/19/2422767/lithuanians-attitudes-towards-migrants-worsen-ukrainians-viewed-most-favourably)
- **European Commission (Oct 2025) summary of the 2024 survey:** a more favourable shift toward labour migrants, with rising support for equal employment opportunities. Acceptance was **79% for foreign students**, **76% for family reunification** and **61% for people displaced by war**, all up on 2023. — [EC Migration & Home Affairs, 24 Oct 2025](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/news/integration-initiatives-and-seasonal-work-increase-lithuania-2025-10-24_en)
- The **2025 Social Attitudes Survey** (Lithuanian Centre for Social Sciences with the NGO Diversity Development Group) assessed how Lithuanians evaluate minority and migrant groups. It is presented in a 2026 *Lituanus* article; **figures not retrieved**. — [LSTC/Lituanus 2026 (PDF)](https://lstc.lt/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Avin-Ileri-Trunov-Lituanus-2026.pdf)
- Policy context: Lithuanian-language requirements for foreign workers were tightened in January 2026. Immigrants often face "social coldness", and social attitudes "lag behind demographic realities". — [Jamestown Foundation, Jan 2026](https://jamestown.org/lithuania-grapples-with-increased-immigration/)
- Net migration was positive for the **fifth year running in 2024 (+23,000)**. — [EMN Lithuania, IOM migration policy 2025 (PDF)](https://www.emn.lt/uploads/Products/product_2103/IOM_politika_2025_FINAL.pdf)
- **Older (flag: 2021):** in an LRT/Norstat poll, **63%** disagreed that Lithuania needs to let in more migrant workers, 29% agreed and 9% had no opinion. — [LRT English, 9 Dec 2021](https://www.lrt.lt/en/news-in-english/19/1559546/despite-labour-shortages-lithuanians-sceptical-about-integrating-migrants-survey)

### Inferences
- Labour immigration, much of it from Central Asia and other non-EU countries, is rising while ethnic distance stays high, so this is a potential flashpoint. The language-requirement tightening in 2026 suggests politicians are responding to public scepticism.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 Eurobarometer figure was found for "immigration" as a national problem in Lithuania.
- The 2025 Social Attitudes Survey percentages were not retrieved.

## A4. Emigration intentions, especially among youth

### Takeaway
I found **no 2025–2026 survey percentage** for youth emigration intentions. The available survey figures are undated or older: 63% of 18–25-year-olds "think about emigration" in an undated Delfi/Spinter poll, while about 16% of young respondents to a CVbankas survey picture their future abroad. Actual flows now favour return migration.

### Cited Findings
- Delfi/Spinter survey (date not shown in extract): about **one in seven residents constantly thinks about emigration**. The **18–25** age group thinks about it most (**63%**), and **40%** overall do not consider leaving. — [anyksta.lt](https://www.anyksta.lt/kas-septintas-gyventojas-nuolat-galvoja-apie-emigracija-rodo-tyrimas)
- CVbankas.lt youth survey (date not shown): about **16%** see their future abroad, while **80%** expect to settle in Lithuania; many want to start a business there. — [CVbankas.lt](https://www.cvbankas.lt/karjeros-patarimas/dauguma-jaunuoliu-ateityje-noretu-likti-lietuvoje-ir-kurti-savo-versla.html)
- Return flows: preliminary Statistics Lithuania data cited by the Foreign Ministry show **18,155 citizens returned and 9,669 left**. The extract referred to "last year", probably 2025, but the **year and URL were not clearly identified**. — [tv3.lt emigracija topic page](https://www.tv3.lt/gaires/emigracija)
- Net migration was positive for the fifth year in 2024 (+23,000). — [EMN Lithuania (PDF)](https://www.emn.lt/uploads/Products/product_2103/IOM_politika_2025_FINAL.pdf)

### Inferences
- The "brain drain" narrative is now more about specific skilled groups, such as medical specialists, and regional depopulation than about mass outflows. Net migration is positive and more citizens return than leave.

### Gaps
- A 2025–2026 representative youth survey on emigration intentions, for example from the Department of Youth Affairs or Eurobarometer youth, was not found.

## A5. Happiness and life-satisfaction rankings

### Takeaway
Life satisfaction is at a **record high: 88% in the autumn 2025 Eurobarometer, above the EU average (86%) for the first time**. Lithuania ranked **#1 for under-30s in the World Happiness Report 2024** (19th overall). In the **WHR 2026**, Lithuania scored **6.704**, about **28th** overall, and is cited among the CEE countries with the largest youth gains. Whether it is still #1 for youth in 2025 or 2026 is **unconfirmed**.

### Cited Findings
- **EB 104 (autumn 2025):** **88%** satisfied with their lives, up 5 points, exceeding the EU average (86%) for the first time. — [lithuania.lt](https://lithuania.lt/governance-in-lithuania/life-satisfaction-in-lithuania-reaches-a-historic-high/)
- Spring 2024 Eurobarometer: 86% happy with their quality of life (older). — [The Baltic Times](https://www.baltictimes.com/86_pct_of_lithuanians_happy_with_their_quality_of_life___eurobarometer); [LRT English, 26 Mar 2024](https://www.lrt.lt/en/news-in-english/19/2234584/majority-of-lithuanians-happy-with-their-quality-of-life-eurobarometer)
- **WHR 2024:** Lithuania was **#1 for under-30s** (youth score **7.76/10**), **#19 overall**, and **#44 for over-60s**. Age-group rankings were new that year. — [Business Insider NL](https://www.businessinsider.nl/lithuania-has-been-ranked-the-happiest-country-in-the-world-for-young-people-data-shows/); [The Nightly](https://thenightly.com.au/lifestyle/lithuania-is-the-happiest-country-for-gen-z-and-millennials-according-to-the-world-happiness-report-c-14080464); [lithuania.lt](https://lithuania.lt/news/life-and-work-in-lithuania/world-happiness-report-lithuanias-youth-leads-the-charge/)
- Reasons cited by young Lithuanians include free university for a large share of students, cheap rent by EU standards and nightlife. A counter-view essay from a Vilnius student points to mental-health gaps. — [Oxford Wellbeing Research Centre](https://wellbeing.hmc.ox.ac.uk/news/lithuania-is-the-happiest-place-in-the-world-for-under-30s-gen-zs-say-low-rent-free-university-and-good-nightlife-are-to-thank/); [Business Insider NL (sceptical essay)](https://businessinsider.nl/i-live-in-lithuania-supposedly-the-worlds-happiest-place-for-gen-z-heres-why-im-not-convinced/)
- **WHR 2026:** Lithuania's score is **6.704**, about **28th**. The aggregator lists number the ranks inconsistently, so **treat the rank as approximate**. The report names Lithuania among CEE countries with some of the largest gains in youth life evaluations since 2006–2010, alongside Serbia, Bulgaria, Latvia and Romania. — [Atlas & Boots](https://www.atlasandboots.com/?p=12864); [AFAR](https://www.afar.com/magazine/the-worlds-happiest-country-is-all-about-reading-coffee-and-saunas?tinycarl=1)
- WHR-based series: Lithuania's score was **6.83 in 2025** and 6.82 in 2024. — [TheGlobalEconomy.com](https://www.theglobaleconomy.com/Lithuania/happiness/)
- Counterpoint: the suicide rate is still the highest in the EU (see B2).

### Inferences
- Lithuania combines high self-reported life satisfaction, especially among the young, with the EU's highest poverty-risk and suicide rates. This "happiness paradox" is shaped by age: the young are very satisfied, while older and rural people carry the poverty burden (65+ poverty risk 39.2%).

### Gaps
- Lithuania's **WHR 2025 overall rank** and its **under-30 rank in WHR 2025 and 2026** were not confirmed from the official worldhappiness.report tables.

## B. Verification of headline figures (confirm / correct / flag)

### Takeaway
Six of the nine figures are **confirmed**: poverty 22.6%, defence 5.38%, shelters about 56%, inflation about 6% in August 2026, the PM, and suicide (with a nuance on the 19.6 vs 18.8 definition). Births of about 17,500 are **essentially confirmed** (sources say 17.5–17.6 thousand). The exact population of **2,886,883 is unconfirmed but consistent** with official rounded figures (2.887–2.888 million). The **31% out-of-pocket share is plausible but rests on older data** (28.7–32%, 2019–2020).

### Cited Findings
**(1) At-risk-of-poverty rate 22.6% in EU-SILC 2025, highest in EU: CONFIRMED.**
- Statistics Lithuania (State Data Agency), reported by LRT/BNS on 31 Mar 2026: the at-risk-of-poverty rate was **22.6% in 2025, up 1.1 points**. About **653,000** people lived below the threshold, which was **€699/month** for a single adult and €1,468 for two adults with two children (+13.5%). By group: **65+: 39.2%** (+2.3 points), **rural 33.4%** (+4 points) vs **urban 17.7%**, single-person households **39.9%**, single adults with children **33.2%**. The average old-age pension (€596.7) equalled 85.4% of the threshold. — [LRT, 31 Mar 2026](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/lietuvoje/2/2884080/skurdo-rizikos-lygis-lietuvoje-pernai-didejo-1-1-punkto)
- Eurostat 2025 flash estimates (via BTA/TPS): **Lithuania highest in the EU at 22.6%**, Latvia 22.0%, Bulgaria 21.2%. In 2024 Latvia (21.6%) was just ahead of Lithuania (21.5%). — [TPS, "Bulgaria's Poverty Rate Falls in 2025, But Remains Among EU's Highest"](https://tps.co.il/articles/bulgarias-poverty-rate-falls-in-2025-but-remains-among-eus-highest/)
