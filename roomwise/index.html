How to take a photo that measures well
  1. Stand in the doorway, or with your back against the opposite wall.
  2. Hold the phone upright at chest height and keep it level.
  3. Fit the whole back wall in the frame, floor to ceiling. In small rooms use the 0.5× lens.

Measure the room

Drag the four yellow corners to where the back wall meets the ceiling and the floor. Then move the eye-level cross to where the room's edges meet.
