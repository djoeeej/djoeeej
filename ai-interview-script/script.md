## 1. Introduction – What is AI? (based on a scientific book)

*Source: Stuart Russell & Peter Norvig, "Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach" (4th ed., Pearson, 2020), the main university textbook on AI. They describe AI as the study of agents that take in information from their environment and act in it. The name "artificial intelligence" was created by John McCarthy in 1955. He described it as "the science and engineering of making intelligent machines."*

**Hello everyone, and welcome.**

**Today our topic is the application of artificial intelligence, or AI: how we use it, which tools we use, and what its dangers are.**

**So what is AI? In the famous textbook *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach*, Stuart Russell and Peter Norvig explain AI as the study of "intelligent agents". An agent is a system that looks at its environment, thinks, and then acts to reach a goal.**

**In simple words: AI is a computer program that learns from a lot of data, finds patterns in it, and uses them to make decisions, answer questions, or create text and images, almost like a human would.**

**The name "artificial intelligence" goes back to 1955. The scientist John McCarthy called it "the science and engineering of making intelligent machines."**

**Today AI is everywhere: in our phones, on social media, in translation apps, and in chatbots like ChatGPT.**

**So let's go into the street and ask people what they think.**

## 5. The Google case – an example of AI misinformation

*Say this to the camera after the interviews.*

**Here is a real example of how dangerous AI mistakes can be, even for one of the biggest companies in the world.**

**In February 2023, Google showed its new AI chatbot, called Bard, in an advertisement.**

**In the ad, someone asked Bard what to tell a 9-year-old about the James Webb Space Telescope.**

**Bard answered that the James Webb Telescope took the very first picture of a planet outside our solar system.**

**But that was false. The first picture of a planet outside our solar system was taken in 2004, almost 20 years earlier, by the Very Large Telescope in Chile, from the European Southern Observatory.**

**Astronomers noticed the mistake on social media very quickly.**

**The next day, the share price of Alphabet, Google's parent company, fell by about 8 percent. In one day, the company lost around 100 billion dollars in market value.**

**So one wrong sentence from an AI chatbot cost Google about 100 billion dollars.**

*Important: Google did NOT pay a 100 billion dollar fine. Nobody fined Google. The company lost about 100 billion dollars in stock market value because investors lost trust. Say "lost", not "paid a fine", so your video is correct.*

## 6. Conclusion

**To conclude: AI is a powerful tool, and as we heard today, many people use it every day.**

**It helps us learn, work, and create faster.**

**But AI is not always right. As the Google example shows, it can say false things with total confidence, and even a giant company can lose billions because of one mistake.**

**So my advice is simple: use AI as a helper, not as the final answer. Always check important information with reliable sources.**
